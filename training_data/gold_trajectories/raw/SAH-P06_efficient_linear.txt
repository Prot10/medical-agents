### Recommendations
1. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, cardiac telemetry, and SBP control targeting <160 mmHg pre-securing
2. Start nimodipine 60 mg orally every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis
3. Urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for aneurysm securing (coiling vs clipping) within 24-72 hours
4. Transfuse packed red blood cells toward an 8-10 g/dL hemoglobin target given anemia's independent contribution to delayed cerebral ischemia risk
5. Monitor serum sodium closely for cerebral salt wasting/SIADH; avoid hypotonic fluids
6. Begin daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 for vasospasm surveillance

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not withhold LP or further workup on the basis of a negative CT alone in a severely anemic patient — CT sensitivity for SAH is markedly reduced when hemoglobin is low
- Do not start aspirin, other antiplatelets, or NSAIDs for headache before the aneurysm is secured — rebleeding risk
- Do not administer nimodipine intravenously — oral/NG only, IV use has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the neurogenic troponin elevation without stronger primary-ACS evidence — this would delay definitive aneurysm treatment
- Do not allow uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160 mmHg) pre-securing, and avoid over-correcting into systemic hypotension
