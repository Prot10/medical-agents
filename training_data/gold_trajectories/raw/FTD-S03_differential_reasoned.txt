### Primary Diagnosis
Behavioral variant frontotemporal dementia (bvFTD), probable, C9orf72-associated (Confidence: 0.80)

### Differential Diagnoses
1. Atypical parkinsonian syndrome (PSP/CBD) - bilateral rigidity is mild and isolated without bradykinesia progression or asymmetric limb signs; imaging shows lobar frontotemporal, not basal ganglia/brainstem, atrophy
2. Alzheimer's disease with frontal presentation - hippocampal volume and posterior parietal/occipital metabolism are preserved, and memory impairment is retrieval- rather than encoding-predominant
3. Late-onset primary psychiatric disorder - frontal release signs, knife-blade gyral atrophy, and hypometabolism are structural/metabolic findings inconsistent with a purely psychiatric process

### Key Evidence
- Bilateral frontal (24% below norms) and anterior temporal (16% below norms) atrophy with posterior sparing from Brain MRI — defines the lobar pattern required for a bvFTD structural diagnosis
- Severe executive/social-cognition deficits with retrieval-pattern memory sparing from neuropsych battery — satisfies the Rascovsky behavioral-cognitive profile
- Pathogenic C9orf72 expansion (~600 repeats) from genetic panel — confirms familial etiology and flags ALS-spectrum risk

### Recommendations
1. Confirm diagnosis via behavioral/cognitive neurology consultation applying Rascovsky 2011 criteria explicitly (document ≥3 of 6 core features within 3 years and functional decline)
2. Screen actively for ALS features at every visit (FVC, fasciculations, dysarthria, bulbar symptoms) given C9orf72 carrier status
3. Initiate genetic counselling and offer cascade testing discussion for at-risk first-degree relatives
4. Assess and document decision-making, financial, and driving capacity given prominent disinhibition and impaired judgment
5. Provide caregiver education, home safety planning, and referral to FTD-focused support resources

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not start acetylcholinesterase inhibitors (donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine) — no benefit in FTD and may worsen behavioral symptoms
- Do not start memantine — no evidence of benefit in bvFTD and may worsen behavioral symptoms
- Avoid antipsychotics as first-line behavioral therapy given increased cerebrovascular and mortality risk in dementia; reserve for refractory dangerous agitation with specialist oversight
- Avoid benzodiazepines for behavioral control — risk of paradoxical disinhibition and falls
