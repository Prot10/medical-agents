### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not diagnose MS from non-specific white-matter lesions alone — this determination rests on meeting McDonald 2017 dissemination-in-space/time criteria plus AQP4/MOG exclusion, not imaging alone
- Do not start natalizumab without JCV serostatus, and do not start fingolimod without baseline ECG/monitoring given PML and bradyarrhythmia risks respectively
- Do not use chronic oral corticosteroids as maintenance MS therapy — reserve steroids for acute relapse only
