### Key Evidence
- Thick basal cisternal blood, Fisher 3 from CT — confirms SAH and rules out alternative headache etiologies
- 6.2 x 5.4 mm lobulated right PComA aneurysm on CTA — identifies the bleeding source and guides clipping vs. coiling planning
- Diffuse T-wave inversions with QTc prolongation, no ST elevation — supports neurogenic stress cardiomyopathy over primary ACS, avoiding an unnecessary cath-lab detour

### Recommendations
1. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, cardiac telemetry, and SBP target generally <160 mmHg pending aneurysm securing
2. Start oral nimodipine 60 mg every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis (oral/NG only)
3. Obtain urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for definitive clipping or coiling within 24-72 hours
4. Monitor serum sodium closely and avoid hypotonic fluids given risk of cerebral salt wasting/SIADH
5. Begin daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 for vasospasm surveillance

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture — CT has already confirmed SAH and blood burden carries herniation risk
- Do not start aspirin, other antiplatelets, or NSAIDs before the aneurysm is secured — increases rebleeding risk
- Do not administer nimodipine intravenously — oral/NG route only, IV use has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the neurogenic troponin elevation — would delay definitive aneurysm treatment without primary-ACS evidence
- Do not allow uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160 mmHg) pre-securing, but avoid over-correcting to systemic hypotension
