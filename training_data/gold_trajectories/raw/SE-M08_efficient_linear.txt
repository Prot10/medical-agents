### Recommendations
1. Administer IV lorazepam 0.1 mg/kg immediately for ongoing NCSE
2. Load levetiracetam as second-line AED — preferred in HIV patients on cART since it is not hepatically metabolized and avoids the CYP3A4 induction seen with phenytoin/carbamazepine
3. Start empiric pyrimethamine + sulfadiazine + leucovorin for presumed toxoplasmosis (note prior sulfonamide allergy — clindamycin substitution for sulfadiazine should be considered) without waiting for further confirmatory testing; reimage at 2 weeks to confirm response
4. Trauma assessment — cervical spine clearance and surgical evaluation of the temporal contusion
5. Continue cART (Biktarvy) without interruption
6. Defer lumbar puncture until mass effect is reassessed as stable; check drug interactions before adding any new agent to the antiretroviral regimen

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not use enzyme-inducing AEDs (phenytoin, carbamazepine) — they reduce cART efficacy via CYP3A4 induction
- Do not perform LP before imaging has characterized the mass effect of the basal ganglia lesion — herniation risk
- Do not delay empiric toxoplasmosis treatment awaiting the 2-week response window before starting therapy
- Do not interrupt cART despite the acute presentation
