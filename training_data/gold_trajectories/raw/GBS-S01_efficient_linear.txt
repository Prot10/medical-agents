2. Initiate IVIG (0.4 g/kg/day x 5 days) or plasma exchange as first-line disease-modifying therapy — check renal function and IgA status (and complete the mandatory drug-interaction check) before administration; do not use corticosteroid monotherapy, which lacks efficacy in GBS.

6. Repeat EMG in 1-2 weeks to clarify AIDP versus evolving axonal (AMAN) injury given the anti-GM1 positivity, which affects prognosis.

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not delay intubation while awaiting further decline — NIF is already at threshold and bulbar weakness increases aspiration risk.
- Do not rely on corticosteroids alone; they are not effective as monotherapy in GBS.
- Avoid neuromuscular blocking agents outside the immediate intubation setting, as underlying weakness makes clearance unpredictable.
- Autonomic instability (bradyarrhythmia, BP lability) is a leading cause of mortality in severe GBS and requires continuous monitoring, not intermittent checks.
