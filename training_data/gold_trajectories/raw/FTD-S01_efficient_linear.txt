2. Initiate formal driving safety and financial/decision-making capacity assessment; restrict access to large financial transactions pending evaluation.

4. Offer genetic counseling and discuss cascade testing given the young age and severity, despite the current negative panel.
5. Consider low-dose SSRI (e.g., sertraline, confirmed safe with current metformin/amlodipine regimen) for disinhibition and compulsive behaviors.

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not start acetylcholinesterase inhibitors or memantine — no benefit in bvFTD and may worsen behavioral symptoms.
- Avoid antipsychotics as first-line behavioral control given increased cerebrovascular and mortality risk in dementia; reserve for refractory dangerous agitation with specialist oversight.
- Avoid benzodiazepines for behavioral symptoms — risk of paradoxical disinhibition and falls.
- Do not attribute the syndrome to alcohol use alone — the structural and metabolic frontotemporal pattern is not explained by alcohol-related cognitive disorder.
