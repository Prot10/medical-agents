### Recommendations
1. IV lorazepam 0.1 mg/kg first-line for ongoing status epilepticus.
2. Hypertonic 3% saline 100 mL bolus for severely symptomatic hyponatremia (Na 124), then rate-limit further correction.
3. Aggressive (active/invasive) cooling for hyperthermia of 40.2°C.
4. IV isotonic fluids for rhabdomyolysis/AKI (CK 8640), monitoring urine output and potassium.
5. Discontinue buspirone and all other serotonergic agents.
6. Cyproheptadine 12 mg PO/NG for severe serotonin syndrome.

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not correct sodium faster than 8 mEq/L per 24 hours after the initial 4-6 mEq bolus correction — risk of osmotic demyelination.
- Do not use antipsychotics for agitation — they worsen hyperthermia and lower seizure threshold.
- Do not rely on cooling blankets alone for temperature >40°C — invasive/active cooling measures are needed.
