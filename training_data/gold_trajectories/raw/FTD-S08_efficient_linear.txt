- Bifrontal and anterior temporal knife-blade atrophy with normal hippocampal volume from Brain MRI — localizes disease to the frontal-anterior-temporal network and argues against AD
- Severely impaired executive function and social cognition (Faux Pas 4/20) with retrieval-pattern memory from neuropsych battery — matches the Rascovsky bvFTD cognitive profile
- Normal metabolic/endocrine labs — excludes reversible contributors

3. Begin caregiver education and safety planning (financial safeguards, home supervision) with referral to FTD-focused caregiver support resources
4. Discuss genetic counseling and possible C9orf72/GRN/MAPT testing with cascade testing for at-risk relatives if a pathogenic variant is later identified
5. For disruptive compulsive/disinhibited behaviors, consider a trial of an SSRI (e.g., sertraline) with slow titration rather than acetylcholinesterase inhibitors, memantine, or antipsychotics

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not start donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine, or memantine — no benefit in bvFTD and may worsen behavioral symptoms
- Avoid antipsychotics as first-line behavioral control given increased cerebrovascular and mortality risk in dementia; reserve for refractory dangerous agitation under specialist oversight
- Avoid benzodiazepines for behavioral symptoms — risk of paradoxical disinhibition and falls
- Delay in addressing legal practice capacity and bar notification risks ongoing harm to clients and professional/legal liability
