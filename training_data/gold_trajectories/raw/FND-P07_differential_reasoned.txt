### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not pursue further negative investigations (e.g., repeat imaging or invasive testing) once positive rule-in signs are documented — this reinforces illness conviction and worsens prognosis
- Do not frame the diagnosis as "nothing wrong" or "all in your head" — this damages the therapeutic alliance and is the most common avoidable harm in FND
- Do not order EMG/NCS or pursue invasive testing (lumbar puncture, biopsy) — not indicated given clear positive rule-in signs and no structural findings
- Do not increase levodopa/pramipexole for the new left-sided tremor — it is not dopaminergic in origin and will not respond, while risking dyskinesia/impulse-control side effects
