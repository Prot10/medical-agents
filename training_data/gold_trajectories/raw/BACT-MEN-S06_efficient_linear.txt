### Recommendations
1. Two sets of peripheral blood cultures should already be drawn (before first antibiotic dose); confirm this occurred
2. Administer empiric IV vancomycin 15-20 mg/kg plus ceftriaxone 2 g within 60 minutes of suspicion — do not await culture confirmation
3. Give dexamethasone 10 mg IV before or with the first antibiotic dose, continued q6h for 4 days
4. Re-evaluate and de-escalate to ceftriaxone monotherapy once susceptibility confirms a penicillin/cephalosporin-susceptible isolate
5. Refer to ENT to evaluate the left mastoid as the likely parameningeal source, given prior otitis media, for possible otomastoiditis requiring drainage
6. Maintain droplet precautions until meningococcal disease is excluded

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not delay antibiotics or dexamethasone while awaiting LP or culture results — every hour of delay increases mortality
- Do not attribute this presentation solely to the prior, treated ear infection; confirmed CSF findings establish active meningitis requiring full treatment regardless of the resolving otitis media
- Do not discontinue dexamethasone early — the 4-day course targets current meningeal inflammation, not the prior ear infection
