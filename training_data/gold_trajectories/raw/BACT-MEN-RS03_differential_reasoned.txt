2. Draw two sets of peripheral blood cultures if not already obtained, prior to any further antibiotic changes

4. Maintain droplet precautions until meningococcal disease is definitively excluded
5. Consider tapering prednisolone in favor of dexamethasone-only corticosteroid coverage per drug-interaction guidance to avoid excess immunosuppression

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not delay antibiotics for imaging or LP beyond the minimum needed for safety; every hour of delay increases mortality
- Do not perform LP before CT in a patient with altered attention or immunosuppression — this order was followed here and should not be reversed in similar future cases
- Do not discontinue dexamethasone based on a negative repeat Gram stain alone; complete the 4-day course in confirmed pneumococcal disease
- Do not treat with oral antibiotics; adequate CNS penetration requires IV therapy
