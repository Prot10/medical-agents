### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not start acetylcholinesterase inhibitors (donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine) — no benefit in FTD and may worsen behavioral symptoms
- Do not start memantine — no evidence of benefit in bvFTD and may worsen behavioral symptoms
- Avoid antipsychotics as first-line behavioral management given increased cerebrovascular and mortality risk in neurodegenerative dementia; reserve for refractory dangerous agitation with specialist oversight
- Avoid benzodiazepines for behavioral control — risk of paradoxical disinhibition and falls
- Do not attribute the ongoing decline solely to valproate hyperammonemia or bipolar relapse — this would delay recognition of the underlying genetic neurodegenerative process and appropriate safety/capacity planning
