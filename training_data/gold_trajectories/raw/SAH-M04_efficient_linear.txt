### Recommendations
1. Start nimodipine 60 mg orally every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis, holding metoprolol if bradycardia develops
2. Urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for clipping versus coiling within 24-72 hours
3. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, SBP target generally <160 mmHg pre-securing, and cardiac telemetry
4. Monitor serum sodium closely for cerebral salt wasting/SIADH and avoid hypotonic fluids
5. Begin daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 for vasospasm surveillance

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not give nimodipine intravenously — IV administration has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not start aspirin, other antiplatelets, or NSAIDs before the aneurysm is secured — raises rebleeding risk
- Do not allow uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160 mmHg) pre-securing, and avoid over-correcting to hypotension
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the troponin elevation without strong primary-ACS evidence — this is expected neurogenic stress cardiomyopathy and workup would delay aneurysm treatment
