### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not use haloperidol or other high-potency D2 antagonists — they can worsen the movement disorder and mimic NMS in this condition.
- Do not delay immunotherapy while awaiting the anti-NMDAR antibody result once clinical probability is high — turnaround can take 1-2 weeks and delay worsens outcomes.
- Do not anchor on a primary psychiatric diagnosis in a young patient with subacute neuropsychiatric symptoms plus seizures or dyskinesias without first excluding autoimmune encephalitis.

- Do not interpret a negative serum antibody as ruling out disease — CSF is the gold-standard sample.
