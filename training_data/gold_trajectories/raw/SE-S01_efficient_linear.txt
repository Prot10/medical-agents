### Red Flags / Alerts

- Avoid carbamazepine, oxcarbazepine, and gabapentin for the same reason
- Do not treat the elevated lactate with bicarbonate — post-convulsive lactic acidosis typically resolves spontaneously once seizures stop
- Do not discharge without addressing medication non-adherence, which is the modifiable driver of this episode
