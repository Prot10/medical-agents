### Recommendations
1. Establish antithrombotic therapy with apixaban (discontinue aspirin per interaction check) while monitoring for hemorrhagic transformation of any evolving infarct

3. Serial neurological checks and repeat vascular imaging to assess dissection evolution and need for endovascular intervention if flow-limiting ischemia progresses

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not dismiss recurrent severe unilateral jaw/face pain as trigeminal neuralgia or dental pathology without cervical vessel imaging — dissection can precede infarct by days
- Do not start full-dose anticoagulation without weighing the intramural hematoma's own hemorrhagic potential against AF-related thromboembolic risk
- Do not assume the mechanism is purely cardioembolic given confirmed dissection — both must be addressed in secondary prevention
