### Recommendations
1. Reverse apixaban immediately with andexanet alfa (standard dose) before any invasive procedure
2. Start nimodipine 60 mg orally every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis; hold metoprolol during the course given additive bradycardia risk
3. Obtain urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for definitive aneurysm securing within 24-72 hours
4. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, cardiac telemetry, and blood pressure control (SBP target generally <160 mmHg pre-securing)
5. Monitor serum sodium closely for cerebral salt wasting/SIADH and avoid hypotonic fluids
6. Begin daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 post-bleed for vasospasm surveillance

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture — CT has already confirmed SAH and the puncture adds no diagnostic value while carrying procedural risk
- Do not start aspirin, other antiplatelets, or NSAIDs for headache before the aneurysm is secured — antiplatelet effect increases rebleeding risk
- Do not give nimodipine intravenously — oral or nasogastric administration only, given reports of fatal cardiovascular collapse with IV use
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the neurogenic troponin elevation — it would delay aneurysm treatment without primary ACS evidence
- Do not proceed to any invasive procedure or aneurysm securing until anticoagulation is reversed and hemostasis confirmed
- Do not allow uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160 mmHg) pre-securing, and avoid over-correcting to systemic hypotension
