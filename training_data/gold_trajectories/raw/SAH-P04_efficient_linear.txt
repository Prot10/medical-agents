### Primary Diagnosis
Severe aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage from AComA aneurysm with hydrocephalus and neurogenic cardiomyopathy (Hunt-Hess 3, Fisher 4) (Confidence: 0.72)

### Differential Diagnoses
1. Hypertensive encephalopathy - does not explain hyperdense subarachnoid and intraventricular blood with hydrocephalus on CT
2. Primary traumatic intracranial hemorrhage from the fall - CT shows no fracture and blood distribution (basal cisterns, Sylvian fissures) is consistent with a ruptured aneurysm as the primary event, trauma secondary
3. Primary acute coronary syndrome accounting for troponin elevation - ECG lacks ST elevation/Q waves and echo shows apical ballooning with basal hyperkinesis, a non-coronary-territory pattern

### Recommendations
1. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, cardiac telemetry, and blood-pressure control (SBP target generally <160 mmHg pre-securing)
2. Start nimodipine 60 mg orally every 4 hours (oral/NG only, never IV) for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis, checking drug interactions against home losartan/clonazepam per hospital formulary policy before administration
3. Urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for definitive clipping vs coiling within 24-72 hours, correcting the INR 1.4 beforehand
4. Emergent neurosurgery consultation for external ventricular drain given the acute hydrocephalus
5. Monitor serum sodium closely for cerebral salt wasting/SIADH and avoid hypotonic fluids
6. Initiate daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 post-bleed for vasospasm surveillance

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture — CT already confirms SAH with intraventricular blood and hydrocephalus, so LP now carries herniation risk
- Do not start aspirin, other antiplatelets, or NSAIDs for headache before the aneurysm is secured — increases rebleeding risk
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the troponin elevation without stronger evidence of primary ACS — would delay definitive aneurysm treatment
- Do not allow uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160 mmHg) pre-securing, but avoid over-correcting to systemic hypotension
- Avoid further benzodiazepines in the neurocritical care unit — they can mask neurological deterioration on serial exams
