1. Continue IV ceftriaxone (de-escalate vancomycin once susceptibility confirmed) with dexamethasone 10 mg IV q6h for a full 4-day course, discontinuing ibuprofen during the steroid course
2. Consult neurosurgery to evaluate shunt patency, since VP shunt patients remain at elevated long-term risk for CNS infection recurrence even when the shunt itself is not the current source
3. Continue droplet precautions until meningococcal disease is definitively excluded by culture/PCR results already trending toward pneumococcus

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not withhold or delay empiric vancomycin/ceftriaxone and dexamethasone for any imaging or CSF result once bacterial meningitis is clinically suspected; the first dose should have been given within 60 minutes of presentation
- Do not discontinue dexamethasone despite a positive Gram stain identifying the organism early — complete the full 4-day course as pneumococcal meningitis is confirmed
- Do not treat with oral antibiotics or rely on a reassuring CT alone to exclude bacterial meningitis in this clinical syndrome
