2. Start nimodipine 60 mg PO every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis; do not give IV
3. Hold hydrochlorothiazide; use IV labetalol or nicardipine if further BP control is needed
4. Urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for aneurysm clipping or coiling within 24-72 hours
5. Monitor serum sodium and lithium levels every 24-48 hours, maintaining euvolemia and avoiding hypotonic fluids
6. Begin daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 for vasospasm surveillance

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture — CT has already confirmed SAH, and LP adds no diagnostic value while carrying procedural risk
- Do not start aspirin, NSAIDs, or other antiplatelet agents before the aneurysm is secured — increases rebleeding risk, which carries roughly 50% mortality
- Do not administer nimodipine intravenously — oral/NG only, IV dosing has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the neurogenic troponin elevation absent strong primary ACS evidence — would delay definitive aneurysm treatment
