### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not initiate or continue antiepileptic drugs for these events — they are ineffective for PNES and expose the patient to unnecessary side effects
- Do not give benzodiazepines during a witnessed event — no benefit for PNES and risk of respiratory depression
- Do not pursue further invasive testing (lumbar puncture, nerve or muscle biopsy) — the positive rule-in signs and video-EEG already establish the diagnosis, and additional negative workups reinforce illness conviction and worsen prognosis
