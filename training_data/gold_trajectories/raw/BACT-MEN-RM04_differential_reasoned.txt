### Recommendations
1. Draw two sets of blood cultures before the first antibiotic dose, then start empiric ceftriaxone 2g IV q12h plus vancomycin 15-20 mg/kg IV q8-12h within 60 minutes of suspicion.
2. Give dexamethasone 10 mg IV before or with the first antibiotic dose, continuing q6h for 4 days, with frequent glucose monitoring given his diabetes.
3. Await CSF culture speciation and MALDI-TOF (standard PCR panels do not cover S. suis) and de-escalate to penicillin G or ceftriaxone monotherapy once susceptibility is confirmed.
4. Arrange audiology follow-up after acute recovery, as S. suis-associated sensorineural hearing loss is frequently permanent.

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not attribute the bilateral hearing loss and dizziness to labyrinthitis and defer lumbar puncture when fever, headache, and meningismus are present.
- Do not delay empiric antibiotics awaiting culture speciation or MALDI-TOF results.
- Do not interpret negative blood cultures as evidence against bacterial meningitis — CSF culture is the confirmatory test, and bacteremia in S. suis is often transient.
- Do not omit adjunctive dexamethasone or give it after rather than before/with the first antibiotic dose.
