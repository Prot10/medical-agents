### Recommendations
1. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, cardiac telemetry, and SBP control (<160 mmHg pre-securing)
2. Start nimodipine 60 mg PO every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm/DCI prophylaxis, holding home antihypertensives during the acute phase and monitoring BP before each dose
3. Urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for aneurysm clipping vs coiling within 24-72 hours; emergent EVD placement for hydrocephalus
4. Serial serum sodium monitoring for cerebral salt wasting/SIADH, avoiding hypotonic fluids
5. Initiate daily transcranial Doppler surveillance for vasospasm days 4-14 post-bleed

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture - CT already confirms SAH with intraventricular blood and hydrocephalus, and LP risks herniation
- Do not start aspirin, other antiplatelets, or NSAIDs before the aneurysm is secured - increases rebleeding risk
- Do not administer nimodipine intravenously - oral/NG route only, IV use has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not allow uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160) or over-correct to hypotension pre-securing
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the neurogenic troponin elevation without strong primary-ACS evidence - this would delay aneurysm treatment
