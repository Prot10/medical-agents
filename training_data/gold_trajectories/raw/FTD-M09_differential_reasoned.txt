### Recommendations

3. Alert family to implement financial safeguards (power of attorney, account monitoring) given documented impulsive financial decisions.

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not discharge with a primary psychiatric or substance-intoxication diagnosis without arranged neurology follow-up — the chronic prodrome requires structural and cognitive workup regardless of ED disposition.
- Avoid benzodiazepines for behavioral control — risk of paradoxical disinhibition and falls in this population.
- Avoid antipsychotics without specialist input given documented increased mortality risk in dementia patients and lack of first-line evidence in bvFTD.
