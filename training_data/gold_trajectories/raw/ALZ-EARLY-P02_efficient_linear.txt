3. Depression-related cognitive complaints — excluded by continued language decline despite sertraline-responsive mood improvement and objective deficits on formal testing

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not attribute the language decline to depression alone — his mood improved with sertraline while language symptoms progressed, and the workup must not stop at a psychiatric explanation
- Avoid anticholinergic medications (e.g., diphenhydramine, oxybutynin, tricyclics), which can worsen cognition in AD
- Avoid benzodiazepines for anxiety or sleep given added fall and cognitive risk
- Do not start antipsychotics absent a clear behavioral indication, given the boxed warning for increased mortality in dementia
- Do not start memantine monotherapy at this mild stage; it is indicated for moderate-to-severe disease
