### Recommendations
1. Administer empiric IV vancomycin 15-20 mg/kg plus ceftriaxone 2 g without further delay, adding ampicillin for Listeria coverage given his age and immunosuppression.
2. Give dexamethasone 10 mg IV with or before the first antibiotic dose, continuing q6h for 4 days, while consolidating/tapering his prednisolone to avoid compounding steroid toxicity.
3. Hold metformin and glipizide given CKD and acute illness; transition to insulin for glucose control.
4. Reassess and de-escalate antibiotics once Gram stain, culture, and PCR/susceptibility results return.
5. Place patient on droplet precautions until meningococcal disease is excluded.

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not withhold or delay antibiotics for further imaging or repeat LP — timing to first dose is the dominant driver of mortality in this syndrome.
- Do not discontinue dexamethasone solely because the initial Gram stain is negative; complete the 4-day course pending organism identification.
- Do not rely on the negative Gram stain to exclude bacterial meningitis — sensitivity is imperfect, especially without prior antibiotic exposure confounding it further.
- Do not treat with oral antibiotics alone; adequate CNS penetration requires IV therapy.
