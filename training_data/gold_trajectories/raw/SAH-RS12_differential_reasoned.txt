- Do not start aspirin, other antiplatelets, or NSAIDs before the aneurysm is secured — raises rebleeding risk, which carries roughly 50% mortality
- Do not give nimodipine intravenously — oral/NG only, as IV dosing has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the troponin elevation absent strong primary ACS evidence — this reflects neurogenic stress cardiomyopathy and catheterization would delay definitive aneurysm treatment
