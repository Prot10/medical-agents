2. Dementia with Lewy bodies - no fluctuating cognition, visual hallucinations, parkinsonism, or RBD, and FDG-PET spares the pattern typically seen in DLB
3. Primary progressive prion disease (e.g., CJD) - RT-QuIC and 14-3-3 negative, and the indolent 18-month course argues strongly against it

- Bilateral parieto-occipital atrophy with hippocampal sparing from MRI — localizes the disease to the dorsal/ventral visual streams rather than medial temporal memory circuits
