### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not attribute the stroke solely to AF — the dissection is a distinct, concurrent mechanism requiring its own antithrombotic strategy
- Do not resume full-dose anticoagulation without weighing the fresh infarct and intramural hematoma for hemorrhagic transformation risk
- Do not dismiss recurrent unilateral jaw/face pain as dental once red-flag vascular risk factors are present — it can precede infarction by days
