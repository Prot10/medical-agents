### Recommendations
1. Start nimodipine 60 mg PO every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis, administered orally/NG only, never IV
2. Obtain urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for definitive aneurysm securing (coiling or clipping) within 24-72 hours
3. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, cardiac telemetry, and blood pressure control targeting SBP <160 mmHg pre-securing
4. Monitor serum sodium closely for cerebral salt wasting/SIADH and avoid hypotonic fluids
5. Begin daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 post-bleed for vasospasm surveillance

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not start aspirin, other antiplatelets, or NSAIDs before the aneurysm is secured — increases rebleeding risk
- Do not perform lumbar puncture — CT is already diagnostic and LP adds no value while risking delay
- Do not administer nimodipine intravenously — oral/NG only, IV use has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the troponin elevation absent strong primary-ACS evidence — this is a neurogenic pattern and catheterization would delay aneurysm treatment
- Do not allow uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160 mmHg) pre-securing, and avoid over-correcting to systemic hypotension
