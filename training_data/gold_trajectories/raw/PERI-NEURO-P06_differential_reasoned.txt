### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not attribute this presentation to IBS or anxiety without checking urine PBG/ALA on a fresh, light-protected sample during an attack
- Avoid all porphyrinogenic drugs (barbiturates, sulfonamides, valproate/most anticonvulsants, rifampin, oral contraceptives, ergots) — these can precipitate a worsening crisis
- Monitor closely for respiratory muscle involvement given active motor axonal denervation; escalate care if bulbar or respiratory weakness emerges
