3. Discuss that concurrent Alzheimer's copathology is common (~50% of iNPH) and may temper but not eliminate cognitive benefit from shunting

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform a large-volume lumbar puncture before MRI has excluded mass effect or obstructive hydrocephalus
- Do not infer iNPH from ventriculomegaly alone without DESH, callosal angle <90°, and a supportive clinical triad
- Do not attribute this presentation to normal aging or deconditioning now that ventriculomegaly and a positive tap test are documented
- A single negative tap test would not exclude iNPH — an extended lumbar drainage trial should be considered in borderline cases
