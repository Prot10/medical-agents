### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not use phenytoin — it blocks the same cardiac sodium channels as the TCA and will worsen QRS widening and cardiotoxicity
- Do not use Class IA/IC or III antiarrhythmics — additive sodium/potassium channel blockade in a TCA-toxic heart
- Do not give flumazenil — risks precipitating refractory seizures in this setting
- Do not give physostigmine for presumed anticholinergic features — contraindicated in TCA overdose, worsens seizures and cardiac toxicity
