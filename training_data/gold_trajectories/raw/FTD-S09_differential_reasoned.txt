### Recommendations
1. Address hyperphagia and weight gain via caregiver-mediated dietary control; start low-dose sertraline for compulsive/behavioral symptoms (AChE inhibitors and memantine contraindicated in bvFTD).
2. Formally document Rascovsky 2011 criteria (≥3 of 6 core features present within the 3-year window) and functional decline (restaurant closure, driving incidents).
3. Assess and document decision-making, financial, and driving capacity; recommend driving cessation given documented crash risk from impulsivity.
4. Initiate advanced-care planning, including long-term care needs and surrogate decision-making.
5. Provide caregiver education, safety planning, and referral to FTD-focused support resources.
6. Offer genetic counselling and discuss cascade testing given the genetic panel results, with follow-up as indicated.

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not start acetylcholinesterase inhibitors or memantine — no benefit in bvFTD and may worsen behavioral symptoms.
- Avoid antipsychotics as first-line for behavioral control given cerebrovascular/mortality risk in dementia; reserve for refractory dangerous agitation with specialist oversight.
- Avoid benzodiazepines for behavioral symptoms — risk of paradoxical disinhibition and falls.
- Do not defer driving-safety discussion — impulsivity-driven crash risk is elevated even with preserved global cognitive scores.
