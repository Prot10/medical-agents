### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not initiate or continue anti-epileptic drugs for these episodes — AEDs are ineffective for PNES and add unnecessary side-effect burden
- Do not give benzodiazepines as abortive therapy for a witnessed event — no benefit and risk of respiratory depression
- Do not pursue further negative invasive testing (EMG/NCS, lumbar puncture) — the rule-in signs and video-EEG capture are already diagnostic, and repeated workups reinforce illness conviction and worsen prognosis
- Do not frame the diagnosis as "nothing wrong" or "all in your head" — this damages the therapeutic alliance and is the most common avoidable harm in FND
