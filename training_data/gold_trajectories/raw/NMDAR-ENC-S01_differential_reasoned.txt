6. Plan for second-line therapy (rituximab and/or cyclophosphamide) if no meaningful clinical improvement by day 10-14.

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not use haloperidol or other high-potency D2 antagonists for agitation — they can worsen the dyskinesias and mimic NMS in this condition.
- Do not delay immunotherapy while awaiting antibody turnaround (which can take 1-2 weeks) given high clinical probability.
- Do not accept a negative serum antibody result as reassuring in isolation — CSF is the definitive compartment and can be positive when serum is negative.
- Do not anchor on a psychiatric diagnosis in a young patient with seizures, dyskinesias, dysautonomia, or CSF pleocytosis without first excluding autoimmune encephalitis.
