### Key Evidence
- Positive Hoover sign and give-way weakness on right hip flexion from bedside exam — classic functional rule-in sign for weakness
- Non-dermatomal sensory loss with sharp midline splitting — inconsistent with any peripheral nerve or cord anatomy
- Forced eye closure and frequency-variable tremor entraining to imposed rhythm during witnessed episodes from bedside exam — rule-in signs for functional tremor/PNES
- Normal brain MRI and normal basic labs from analyze_brain_mri / interpret_labs — excludes structural and metabolic mimics
- Video-EEG capturing five habitual events with no ictal correlate and normal interictal background from analyze_eeg — diagnostic for PNES
- Elevated PHQ-9/GAD-7/PCL-5 with passed symptom validity testing from neuropsych battery — confirms genuine (non-feigned) distress relevant to treatment, not diagnosis

### Recommendations
1. Communicate the diagnosis transparently and positively, anchoring on the Hoover sign, tremor entrainment, and diagnostic video-EEG — explicitly affirm the symptoms are real and involuntary
2. Refer to FND-informed physiotherapy for the functional motor weakness
3. Refer to trauma-focused CBT / psychological therapy for PNES and comorbid PTSD, depression, and anxiety
4. Continue sertraline; reassess dose with psychiatry given comorbid PTSD/MDD/GAD burden, checking interactions before any change

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not pursue further negative investigations (e.g., EMG/NCS, lumbar puncture) now that positive rule-in signs and a diagnostic video-EEG are present — additional negative workup reinforces illness conviction and worsens prognosis
- Do not tell the patient "nothing is wrong" or "it's all in your head" — this damages engagement and is the most common avoidable harm in FND
- Do not start or continue antiepileptic drugs for the shaking episodes — they are non-epileptic and AEDs offer no benefit while adding side-effect risk
- Do not give benzodiazepines as abortive therapy during witnessed events — no benefit and unnecessary sedation/respiratory risk
