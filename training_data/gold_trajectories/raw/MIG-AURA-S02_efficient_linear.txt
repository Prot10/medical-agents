### Primary Diagnosis
Migraine with sensory (somatosensory) aura, menstrually-related (ICHD-3 1.2.1) (Confidence: 0.90)

### Differential Diagnoses
1. Focal (sensory) seizure — symptom spread over seconds, not the 15-25 minute march seen here
2. TIA / embolic event — onset is abrupt/maximal at onset, not a gradual march; no vascular risk factors, normal MRI
3. Structural or demyelinating lesion — MRI shows no periventricular/juxtacortical lesion or mass, only an incidental punctate WMH

### Key Evidence
- 15-25 minute hand-to-face sensory march followed by unilateral throbbing headache with photophobia/phonophobia — from history, hallmark of cortical spreading depression
- Strict clustering with menstrual cycle over 3 years, family history of similar episodes — supports menstrual migraine pattern
- Normal brain MRI with normal DWI — from analyze_brain_mri, excludes structural/ischemic mimic
- Sumatriptan confirmed appropriate and effective — from check_drug_interactions, supports migraine-specific therapy response

### Recommendations
1. Confirm and document ICHD-3 1.2 criteria for migraine with sensory aura in the chart
2. Have patient keep a headache diary to document the menstrual trigger pattern and attack timing relative to cycle
3. Continue sumatriptan 100 mg at headache onset (not during aura) for acute attacks
4. Start perimenstrual mini-prophylaxis (e.g., frovatriptan or naproxen for 4-5 days around expected menses) given the tightly clustered menstrual pattern [Holland_2012]
5. Counsel explicitly against combined oestrogen-progestogen contraception (WHO Category 4 in migraine with aura) if contraception is desired, and discuss progestogen-only or non-hormonal alternatives

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not start combined oestrogen-progestogen contraception — contraindicated (WHO Category 4) in migraine with aura due to elevated ischemic stroke risk
- Do not co-administer triptans with ergotamines — risk of prolonged vasospasm
- Advise return if aura pattern changes (new weakness, aphasia, aura >60 minutes, or headache onset without aura) as these would warrant re-evaluation
