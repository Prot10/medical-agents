### Recommendations
1. Obtain CTA head and neck immediately to characterize the aneurysm and guide neurosurgery/interventional neuroradiology consult for clipping versus coiling within 24-72 hours
2. Start nimodipine 60 mg PO every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis
3. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, SBP target <160 mmHg pre-securing, and cardiac telemetry
4. Hold HCTZ during acute SAH; monitor serum sodium and lithium levels every 24-48h given hyponatremia risk raising lithium toxicity
5. Begin daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 post-bleed for vasospasm surveillance

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture — CT already confirms SAH and adds herniation risk without diagnostic benefit
- Do not start aspirin, other antiplatelets, or NSAIDs before the aneurysm is secured — increases rebleeding risk
- Do not administer nimodipine intravenously — oral/NG only, IV use has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the neurogenic troponin/ECG pattern without strong independent ACS evidence — would delay aneurysm securing
- Avoid uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160) pre-securing, but do not over-correct to systemic hypotension
