5. Begin early physical and occupational therapy once stabilized; repeat EMG/NCS at 1-2 weeks to reassess for secondary axonal injury and refine prognosis.

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not treat with corticosteroid monotherapy — it is not effective in GBS and delays proven immunotherapy.
- Do not delay intubation awaiting further FVC decline — she already meets criteria for impending failure with bulbar compromise and aspiration risk.
