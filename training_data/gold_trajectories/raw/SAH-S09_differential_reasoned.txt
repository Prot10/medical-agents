### Recommendations

3. Urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for clipping versus coiling within 24-72 hours
4. Hold metformin around contrast administration and monitor glucose and renal function closely; avoid hypotonic fluids and monitor sodium for cerebral salt wasting/SIADH
5. Initiate daily transcranial Doppler days 4-14 for vasospasm surveillance

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture — CT has already confirmed SAH, and LP adds no diagnostic value while carrying herniation risk
- Do not start aspirin or other antiplatelet/NSAID therapy for headache before the aneurysm is secured — raises rebleeding risk
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the neurogenic troponin elevation without stronger primary-ACS evidence — would delay definitive aneurysm treatment
- Do not administer nimodipine intravenously — oral/NG only, IV use has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Avoid uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160 mmHg) pre-securing, but also avoid over-correcting to systemic hypotension given additive effects with nimodipine
