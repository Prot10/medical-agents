### Recommendations
1. Immediate decontamination — remove clothing and wash skin to halt ongoing absorption
2. Atropine IV titrated to drying of bronchial secretions, not heart rate, with escalating doses as needed
3. Pralidoxime 1-2 g IV as early as possible, before enzyme aging reduces efficacy
4. Benzodiazepines (lorazepam/diazepam) as first-line for seizure control
5. Mechanical ventilation with rocuronium (not succinylcholine) for intubation
6. Anticipate intermediate syndrome at 24-96 hours and monitor respiratory function serially; treat hypoglycemia and hypokalemia

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not use succinylcholine for intubation — pseudocholinesterase inhibition causes prolonged paralysis
- Do not use phenytoin as an antiseizure agent — ineffective against the cholinergic seizure mechanism
- Avoid morphine — worsens bronchorrhea and respiratory depression in cholinergic crisis
- Do not extubate prematurely — intermediate syndrome can cause delayed respiratory failure at 24-96 hours
