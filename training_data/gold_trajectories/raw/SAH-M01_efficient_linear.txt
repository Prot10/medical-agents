### Recommendations
1. Start nimodipine 60 mg orally every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis
2. Obtain urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for aneurysm securing (coiling or clipping) within 24-72 hours
3. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, cardiac telemetry, and blood pressure control (SBP generally <160 mmHg pre-securing)
