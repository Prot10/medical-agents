### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform large-volume lumbar puncture before imaging has excluded mass effect or obstructive hydrocephalus
- Do not infer iNPH from ventriculomegaly alone — DESH, a callosal angle <90°, and the clinical triad were all required and confirmed here
- Do not diagnose Alzheimer's disease in a gait-predominant presentation with ventriculomegaly without first excluding NPH, as was done here
- Do not attribute this presentation to normal aging or deconditioning now that imaging and tap-test criteria for NPH are met
