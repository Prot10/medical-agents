### Recommendations
1. Do not perform lumbar puncture — CT already confirms SAH; proceed directly to definitive management
2. Start nimodipine 60 mg orally every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis, given only by oral/NG route
3. Obtain urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for clipping versus coiling within 24-72 hours
4. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, cardiac telemetry, SBP target generally <160 mmHg pre-securing, and close sodium monitoring; avoid hypotonic fluids
5. Check drug interactions before prescribing nimodipine — hold verapamil (additive calcium-channel effect) and hold sertraline (QTc 516 ms is a critical threshold) per medication review, and correct potassium/magnesium for QT protection
6. Begin daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 post-bleed for vasospasm surveillance

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not start aspirin, NSAIDs, or other antiplatelet agents before the aneurysm is secured — increases rebleeding risk
- Do not give nimodipine intravenously — oral/NG only; IV administration has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the neurogenic troponin elevation without strong primary-ACS evidence — this would delay aneurysm treatment
- Do not allow uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160 mmHg) pre-securing, and avoid over-correcting to systemic hypotension
- Continue sertraline and verapamil only after formal interaction review — QTc 516 ms plus combined QT-prolonging and cardiodepressant effects carries torsades and hemodynamic risk
