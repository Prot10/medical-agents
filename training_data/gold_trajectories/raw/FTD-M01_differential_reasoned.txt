### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not start acetylcholinesterase inhibitors (donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine) — no benefit in bvFTD and may worsen behavioral symptoms
- Do not start memantine — no evidence of benefit and may worsen behavior
- Avoid antipsychotics as first-line behavioral management — increased cerebrovascular and mortality risk in dementia; reserve for refractory dangerous agitation with specialist oversight
- Avoid benzodiazepines for behavioral control — risk of paradoxical disinhibition and falls
