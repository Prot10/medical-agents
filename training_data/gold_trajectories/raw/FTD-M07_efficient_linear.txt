### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not attribute the entire syndrome to alcohol use — the disproportionate frontal-lobar atrophy and behavioral phenotype indicate a primary neurodegenerative process requiring its own management and disclosure.
- Do not start cholinesterase inhibitors or memantine — no benefit in bvFTD and may worsen behavioral symptoms.
- Avoid antipsychotics as first-line behavioral management given elevated cerebrovascular and mortality risk in dementia; reserve for refractory dangerous agitation with specialist oversight.
- Avoid benzodiazepines for behavioral control — risk of paradoxical disinhibition and falls, and caution needed even for withdrawal management given the cognitive syndrome.
