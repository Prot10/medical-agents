Functional neurological disorder — functional left hemiparesis (Confidence: 0.90)

### Differential Diagnoses
1. Acute ischemic stroke — normal DWI/ADC across vascular territories and no signal abnormality excludes this
2. Demyelinating event (e.g., MS relapse) — no T2/FLAIR lesion or enhancement on MRI
3. Structural mass or compressive lesion — no mass effect or abnormal enhancement seen

### Key Evidence
- Positive Hoover sign (involuntary hip extension on contralateral resistance testing) from bedside exam — reliable rule-in sign for functional leg weakness
- Distractibility of arm weakness (2/5 formal vs 4/5 with arithmetic task) — inconsistency is a positive functional finding
- Non-dermatomal sensory loss splitting exactly at midline — incompatible with a single anatomic lesion
- Normal gait observed unobserved vs. dragging when observed — further inconsistency supporting a functional mechanism
- Normal brain MRI (analyze_brain_mri) — excludes structural/ischemic/demyelinating cause
- Normal labs (interpret_labs) — excludes metabolic/endocrine mimic

### Recommendations
1. Communicate the diagnosis directly and positively: explain that FND is a recognized, common neurological condition, that her symptoms are real and involuntary, and anchor the explanation on the specific rule-in signs (Hoover sign, distractibility) rather than "nothing was found"
2. Refer to FND-informed physiotherapy targeting normal movement patterns and to CBT/psychological therapy, given her acute stressor, prior social anxiety disorder, and eating disorder history
3. Order a comprehensive neuropsychological battery to characterize comorbid depression, anxiety, somatization, and trauma exposure, to inform the treatment plan
4. Screen current medications (escitalopram) for interactions before adjusting or adding any psychiatric treatment

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not pursue further "ever-expanding" negative investigations (e.g., EMG/NCS, lumbar puncture, repeat imaging) — with clear positive rule-in signs and a normal MRI, additional negative testing only reinforces illness conviction and worsens prognosis
- Do not frame the diagnosis as "nothing is wrong" or "it's all in your head" — this damages the therapeutic alliance and is the most common avoidable harm in FND communication
