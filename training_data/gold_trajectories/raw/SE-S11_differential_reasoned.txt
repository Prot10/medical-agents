### Recommendations
1. Administer IV lorazepam 0.1 mg/kg immediately as first-line abortive therapy for ongoing SE
2. Treat hyperkalemia urgently: calcium gluconate IV for cardiac membrane stabilization, then insulin + dextrose, sodium bicarbonate, and sodium polystyrene for interim K+ lowering
3. Arrange emergent hemodialysis without delay — the only definitive treatment for uremic SE, hyperkalemia, and the metabolic acidosis
4. Correct severe hypocalcemia (Ca 6.8) with IV calcium replacement in parallel with hyperkalemia treatment
5. Load IV levetiracetam at renal-adjusted dose (500-1000 mg, not standard 1500 mg), with supplemental dosing after dialysis
6. Control blood pressure (currently 198/118) carefully alongside acute seizure and electrolyte management

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not delay emergent hemodialysis to pursue additional workup — it is the definitive treatment and further delay allows ongoing uremic/electrolyte injury
- Do not dose antiepileptics (e.g., levetiracetam) at standard renal-normal doses — accumulation risk is high with essentially no renal clearance
- Do not give IV phosphate or magnesium supplementation without dialysis imminently available — will worsen an already critical retention state in ESRD
