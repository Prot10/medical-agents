### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not pursue tilt-table testing — it evaluates autonomic reflex syncope, not exertional catecholaminergic VT, and would delay definitive therapy
- Avoid sympathomimetic agents and QT-prolonging drugs, which can precipitate malignant arrhythmia in this substrate
- Do not attribute the limb jerking to a seizure disorder or start antiepileptic drugs — the pattern is convulsive syncope secondary to cerebral hypoperfusion
- Do not clear for sport or strenuous activity until beta-blocker therapy is established and tolerated
