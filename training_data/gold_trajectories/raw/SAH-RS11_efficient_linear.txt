### Recommendations
1. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, telemetry, and SBP control generally <160 mmHg pre-securing
2. Start nimodipine 60 mg orally every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis (oral only, never IV)
3. Obtain urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for clipping versus coiling within 24-72 hours
4. Monitor serum sodium closely for cerebral salt wasting/SIADH and avoid hypotonic fluids
5. Initiate daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 post-bleed for vasospasm surveillance

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture — CT already confirms SAH, and LP adds herniation risk without diagnostic benefit
- Do not start aspirin, other antiplatelets, or NSAIDs before the aneurysm is secured — rebleeding risk is markedly increased
- Do not administer nimodipine intravenously — oral/NG only, IV use has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the neurogenic troponin elevation absent strong primary ACS evidence — this would delay definitive aneurysm treatment
