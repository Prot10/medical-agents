- Positive urine cocaine metabolite with severe hypertension on arrival — supports cocaine as the precipitant of aneurysm rupture

### Recommendations
1. Start nimodipine 60 mg PO q4h for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis
2. Urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for clipping versus coiling within 24-72 hours
3. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, cardiac telemetry, and SBP target generally <160 mmHg pre-securing using nicardipine (avoid pure beta-blockers given recent cocaine use)
4. Daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 for vasospasm surveillance; monitor serum sodium closely and avoid hypotonic fluids

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture — CT already confirms SAH and LP adds only herniation risk without diagnostic benefit
- Do not start aspirin, NSAIDs, or pursue cardiac catheterization for the troponin elevation before the aneurysm is secured — antiplatelet effects and catheterization delays raise rebleeding risk from a non-ACS process

- Avoid pure beta-blockade for BP control given recent cocaine use — risk of unopposed alpha stimulation and paradoxical hypertension
