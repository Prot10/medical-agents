### Recommendations

2. Arrange permanent pacemaker implantation (Class I indication for symptomatic complete heart block)
3. Hold metoprolol and any other AV-nodal blocking agents pending pacing
4. Counsel on driving restriction until pacemaker is placed and symptoms resolve

### Red Flags / Alerts

- Do not discharge without a definitive pacing plan in place
