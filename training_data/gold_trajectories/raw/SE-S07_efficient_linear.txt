### Recommendations
1. Administer IV lorazepam 0.1 mg/kg as first-line abortive therapy for ongoing SE
2. Reload IV levetiracetam to a therapeutic level, adding a second-line agent such as lacosamide if seizures persist
3. Re-escalate dexamethasone back toward the prior effective dose to address worsening peritumoral edema
4. Arrange a multidisciplinary discussion with neuro-oncology and palliative care to align seizure and tumor-directed management with the patient's goals of care given progressive disease

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture — mass effect with leftward midline shift carries herniation risk
- Do not use enzyme-inducing AEDs such as phenytoin or carbamazepine — they reduce temozolomide exposure and could compromise tumor treatment
- Do not escalate to continuous anesthetic infusion for refractory SE without first clarifying goals of care given the progressive, treatment-refractory GBM
