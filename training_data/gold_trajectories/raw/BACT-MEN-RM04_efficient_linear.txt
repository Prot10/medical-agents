### Recommendations
1. Continue empiric IV ceftriaxone 2g q12h plus vancomycin 15-20 mg/kg IV q8-12h, already started within 60 minutes of suspicion without waiting for culture speciation
2. Continue adjunctive dexamethasone 10 mg IV q6h for 4 days, given before/with the first antibiotic dose
3. Monitor blood glucose closely and hold metformin during acute illness given dexamethasone's hyperglycemic effect
4. Await CSF culture speciation/MALDI-TOF (multiplex PCR panels do not cover S. suis) and de-escalate to penicillin G or ceftriaxone monotherapy once susceptibility is confirmed
5. Arrange audiology follow-up given the high likelihood of persistent sensorineural hearing loss

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not attribute bilateral hearing loss and vertigo to labyrinthitis and defer lumbar puncture when fever, headache, and meningismus are present
- Do not delay empiric antibiotics or dexamethasone awaiting CSF culture speciation results
- Do not interpret negative blood cultures as evidence against bacterial meningitis — bacteremia in S. suis is frequently transient
- Do not treat with oral antibiotics alone; adequate CNS penetration requires IV therapy
