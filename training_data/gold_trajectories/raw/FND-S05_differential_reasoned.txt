### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not pursue further negative investigations (e.g., EMG/NCS, lumbar puncture, repeat imaging) — with positive rule-in signs already present, additional testing only reinforces illness conviction and worsens prognosis.
- Do not tell the patient "nothing is wrong" or that it is "all in her head" — this framing damages the therapeutic alliance and is the most common avoidable harm in FND care.
- Do not start an anticonvulsant for these events — AEDs are ineffective for PNES and expose the patient to unnecessary side effects.
- Do not give benzodiazepines as abortive therapy for future witnessed events — no benefit for PNES and risk of respiratory depression.
