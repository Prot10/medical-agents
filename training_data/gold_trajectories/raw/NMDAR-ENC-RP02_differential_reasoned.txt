### Recommendations
1. Permanently discontinue nivolumab and start high-dose IV methylprednisolone 1 g daily for 5 days with IVIG 0.4 g/kg/day for 5 days (or plasma exchange) without waiting for further confirmatory testing
2. Continue empiric IV acyclovir until CSF HSV/VZV studies and cultures are fully finalized, then discontinue
3. Admit to ICU with continuous EEG monitoring, aggressive seizure control, and close monitoring for dysautonomia and hypoventilation; escalate to rituximab and/or cyclophosphamide if no improvement by day 10-14
4. Treat the newly identified aspiration pneumonia and correct the mild hyponatremia slowly, recognizing it as an SIADH-type feature of the encephalitis rather than its primary driver

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not give haloperidol or other high-potency D2 antagonists for agitation — they can worsen the movement disorder and mimic neuroleptic malignant syndrome
- Do not delay immunotherapy while awaiting further antibody confirmation given the high clinical probability already established
- Do not correct the mild hyponatremia rapidly or treat it as the cause of the encephalopathy — it is a recognized SIADH-mediated feature of the disease
