### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture — CT already confirms SAH, and it adds herniation risk with no diagnostic benefit
- Do not start aspirin or other antiplatelets, or NSAIDs for headache, before the aneurysm is secured — raises rebleeding risk
- Do not give nimodipine intravenously — oral/NG only, IV use has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the neurogenic troponin elevation — would delay definitive aneurysm treatment
- Do not allow uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160) pre-securing, but avoid over-correcting to hypotension
