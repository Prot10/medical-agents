### Primary Diagnosis
Dementia with Lewy bodies (DLB) (Confidence: 0.72)

### Differential Diagnoses
1. Parkinson's disease dementia — excluded by timing, since cognitive fluctuation and hallucinations preceded motor onset by about a year rather than following a long motor-first course
2. Alzheimer's disease with psychotic features — argued against by preserved hippocampal volume, spared verbal memory, and occipital (not temporoparietal) hypometabolism
3. Multiple system atrophy — autonomic failure and mild ataxic features are present, but the early, prominent hallucinations, fluctuating cognition, and RBD are not typical MSA features

### Key Evidence
- Reduced striatal DAT uptake from DaTscan — confirms a true nigrostriatal dopaminergic deficit rather than a mimicking parkinsonism
- Occipital hypometabolism with positive cingulate island sign on FDG-PET — a pattern distinguishing DLB from Alzheimer's
- REM sleep without atonia confirmed on polysomnography — objective evidence of RBD, a core diagnostic feature
- Visuospatial/executive-predominant neuropsych profile with spared memory — matches the expected DLB cognitive signature
- Cardiac MIBG denervation — supportive biomarker consistent with a Lewy body process
- History of severe rigidity/obtundation after haloperidol — characteristic neuroleptic sensitivity reaction

### Recommendations
1. Start a cholinesterase inhibitor (rivastigmine or donepezil, titrated slowly) for cognitive fluctuation and hallucinations
2. If pharmacologic treatment of hallucinations/agitation becomes necessary, restrict to quetiapine, clozapine, or pimavanserin
3. Continue midodrine and counsel on non-pharmacologic measures for orthostatic hypotension; reassess fall risk
4. Counsel the family on caregiver support, safety planning around fluctuating cognition and hallucinations, and RBD-related injury prevention (bedroom safety measures)

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not rechallenge with haloperidol or any typical/most atypical antipsychotic (risperidone, olanzapine, aripiprazole) — severe neuroleptic sensitivity reaction already documented
- Do not start centrally acting anticholinergics (trihexyphenidyl, benztropine) — will worsen cognition and hallucinations in this population
- Do not treat nausea or GI symptoms with metoclopramide or prochlorperazine — both are dopamine antagonists that can precipitate the same severe parkinsonian reaction
