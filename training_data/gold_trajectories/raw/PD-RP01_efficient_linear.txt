### Recommendations
1. Apply MDS 2015 criteria (bradykinesia plus rigidity, no absolute exclusion criteria, supportive prodromal features) to confirm PD diagnosis and document the temporal sequence of motor and non-motor features in the chart
2. Co-manage with cardiology to formally exclude a residual WPW-related arrhythmic contribution to syncope, given his conduction disease history
3. Treat orthostatic hypotension with non-pharmacologic measures first (compression stockings, liberalized salt/fluid, head-of-bed elevation), escalating to midodrine, droxidopa, or fludrocortisone if needed
4. Initiate carbidopa/levodopa cautiously with close orthostatic BP monitoring given additive hypotension risk with amlodipine
5. Establish neuropsychological baseline now for longitudinal surveillance of conversion to PD dementia or DLB
