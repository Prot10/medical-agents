### Key Evidence
- Positive Hoover sign (left hip extension normalizes with contralateral resisted flexion) from bedside exam — a validated positive rule-in sign for functional leg weakness.
- Arm weakness inconsistent under distraction (4/5 vs 2/5) — give-way pattern typical of functional weakness rather than a fixed pyramidal deficit.
- Non-dermatomal sensory loss with abrupt midline split — anatomically implausible for a single structural lesion.
