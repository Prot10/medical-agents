### Recommendations
1. Urgent neurosurgery and interventional neuroradiology consultation for definitive aneurysm securing (clipping or coiling) within 24-72 hours
2. Start nimodipine 60 mg orally every 4 hours for 21 days for vasospasm prophylaxis
3. Admit to neurocritical care with continuous neuro checks, cardiac telemetry, and BP control targeting SBP <160 mmHg pre-securing
4. Monitor serum sodium closely for cerebral salt wasting/SIADH; avoid hypotonic fluids
5. Initiate daily transcranial Doppler monitoring days 4-14 post-bleed for vasospasm surveillance
6. Coordinate with obstetrics/maternal-fetal medicine to manage co-existing eclampsia/PRES with magnesium and BP control alongside aneurysm securing

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not perform lumbar puncture - CT already confirms SAH, and LP adds herniation risk without diagnostic benefit
- Do not start aspirin or other antiplatelet/NSAID agents before the aneurysm is secured - increases rebleeding risk
- Do not administer nimodipine intravenously - oral/NG route only, IV use has caused fatal cardiovascular collapse
- Do not pursue cardiac catheterization for the neurogenic troponin elevation without stronger primary-ACS evidence - would delay aneurysm treatment
- Do not allow uncontrolled hypertension (SBP >160 mmHg) pre-securing, and avoid over-correcting to systemic hypotension
