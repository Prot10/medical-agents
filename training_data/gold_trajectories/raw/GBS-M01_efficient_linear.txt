### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not use corticosteroid monotherapy — ineffective in GBS and may worsen outcome
- Do not sequence IVIG followed by plasma exchange — no additive benefit and increased adverse events
- Avoid sedating or respiratory-depressant medications while FVC is trending toward the intubation threshold
- Confirm renal function and IgA levels before starting IVIG to avoid nephrotoxicity or anaphylactoid reaction
