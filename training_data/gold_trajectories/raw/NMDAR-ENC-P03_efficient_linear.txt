### Recommendations
1. Start first-line immunotherapy now — IV methylprednisolone 1 g daily x5 days with IVIG 0.4 g/kg/day x5 days (or plasma exchange); do not wait for confirmatory antibody titers that already returned
2. Continue empiric IV acyclovir until HSV PCR negativity is documented, then may discontinue
3. Optimize seizure control with levetiracetam (uptitrate) or add valproate, guided by continuous EEG
4. Admit to ICU for autonomic instability monitoring; plan early escalation to rituximab/cyclophosphamide if no improvement by day 10-14
5. Repeat tumor surveillance imaging in 3-6 months given ongoing (though currently negative) paraneoplastic risk

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not use haloperidol or other D2 antagonists for agitation/dyskinesias — risk of worsening extrapyramidal symptoms and NMS-like reaction
- Do not delay immunotherapy pending further antibody confirmation — turnaround delays worsen functional outcome
- Do not anchor on relapse or psychiatric diagnosis alone — autoimmune encephalitis must be excluded first in this presentation
- Do not repeat lumbar puncture reflexively without reassessing for mass effect if consciousness worsens further
