### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not start acetylcholinesterase inhibitors or memantine — no evidence of benefit in bvFTD and may worsen behavioral symptoms
- Avoid antipsychotics as first-line for behavioral symptoms given increased cerebrovascular and mortality risk in dementia; reserve for refractory dangerous agitation with specialist oversight
- Avoid benzodiazepines for behavioral control — risk of paradoxical disinhibition and falls
- Mild bilateral rigidity alone does not warrant a DaTscan in the absence of an asymmetric parkinsonian syndrome
