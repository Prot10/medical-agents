2. Primary psychiatric disorder (catatonia/first-episode psychosis) - CSF pleocytosis, oligoclonal bands, and electrographic seizures indicate an organic CNS process
3. Other autoimmune/paraneoplastic limbic encephalitis (e.g., anti-LGI1, anti-GABA-B) - clinical phenotype (orofacial dyskinesias, young woman, dysautonomia) and CSF antibody result are specific for NMDAR rather than these

- CSF anti-NMDA receptor (GluN1) IgG positive, titer 1:320 from analyze_csf — gold-standard confirmatory test, obtained on CSF rather than serum alone
- Extreme delta brush pattern from analyze_eeg — highly specific EEG signature supporting the diagnosis independent of antibody turnaround time

### Recommendations
1. Send paired CSF and serum anti-NMDAR (GluN1) IgG if not already resulted from both compartments; treat the CSF result as definitive regardless of serum titer.
2. Continue empiric IV acyclovir 10 mg/kg q8h until HSV PCR (already negative) is formally finalized and clinical suspicion is closed out.
3. Begin first-line immunotherapy now — IV methylprednisolone 1 g daily x5 days combined with IVIG 0.4 g/kg/day x5 days (or plasma exchange) — without waiting further for confirmatory antibody titers, given the strength of the current evidence.
4. Obtain pelvic imaging (transvaginal ultrasound and/or contrast-enhanced CT chest/abdomen/pelvis) to screen for ovarian teratoma; proceed to gynecologic-oncology resection if identified.
5. Continue continuous EEG monitoring and treat with levetiracetam or valproate to control both clinical and electrographic seizures.
6. If no meaningful clinical improvement by day 10-14 of first-line therapy, escalate to second-line immunotherapy (rituximab 375 mg/m² weekly x4 and/or cyclophosphamide), with hepatitis B/C and TB screening beforehand per formulary safety protocol.

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not treat this as a primary psychiatric illness and withhold immunotherapy — the CSF and EEG findings indicate an organic autoimmune process requiring urgent treatment.
- Do not use haloperidol or other high-potency D2 antagonists for agitation; they can worsen the dyskinesias and mimic neuroleptic malignant syndrome in this condition.
- Do not delay immunotherapy pending a formal serum antibody result — CSF positivity already meets the threshold to treat, and antibody turnaround can take one to two weeks.
- Do not interpret any negative serum antibody test as excluding the diagnosis; the CSF assay is the reliable one here.
