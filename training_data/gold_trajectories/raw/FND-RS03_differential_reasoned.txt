### Recommendations
1. Communicate the diagnosis directly and transparently using positive language, anchoring the explanation on the rule-in exam findings and video-EEG results, emphasizing the symptoms are real and involuntary
2. Refer for FND-informed physiotherapy targeting the functional limb weakness and gait pattern
3. Refer for CBT/psychological therapy for PNES and comorbid anxiety
4. Obtain a comprehensive neuropsychological battery to characterize comorbid mood, anxiety, and trauma exposure and inform treatment planning
5. Taper any AEDs if previously started for the presumed seizure disorder, as they are not effective for PNES

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not pursue further invasive or repeat negative investigations (e.g., lumbar puncture, EMG/NCS, repeat imaging) once positive rule-in signs and video-EEG confirmation are established — additional negative workups reinforce illness conviction and worsen prognosis
- Do not frame the diagnosis as "nothing is wrong" or "it's all in your head" — this damages therapeutic engagement and is the most common avoidable harm in FND
- Do not initiate or continue long-term antiepileptic drugs for these events, and do not use benzodiazepines as abortive therapy for witnessed episodes
