### Recommendations
1. Treat the acute optic neuritis relapse with high-dose IV methylprednisolone 1 g daily for 3-5 days; reserve plasma exchange for steroid-refractory severe visual loss
2. After relapse stabilization, initiate a disease-modifying therapy, counseling on platform versus high-efficacy agent choice based on lesion burden, JC virus serostatus, and reproductive plans; check JCV antibody index before considering natalizumab, and confirm contraception before teriflunomide or obtain baseline ECG before fingolimod
3. Give all age-appropriate live-attenuated vaccines at least 4 weeks before starting immunosuppressive therapy
4. Supplement vitamin D to therapeutic levels and counsel on Uhthoff phenomenon, heat avoidance, and smoking cessation

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not start a disease-modifying therapy before mimic exclusion is complete — natalizumab in particular can worsen NMOSD relapses if that diagnosis were missed
- Do not use chronic oral corticosteroids as maintenance therapy — they do not prevent relapse or progression and carry long-term harm
- Do not initiate fingolimod without a baseline ECG and first-dose bradyarrhythmia monitoring, and do not administer live vaccines once a B-cell depleting or S1P-modulator therapy has started
