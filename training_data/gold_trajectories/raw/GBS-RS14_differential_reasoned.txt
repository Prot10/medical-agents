1. Cervical myelopathy / transverse myelitis - no sensory level, no bowel/bladder involvement, and normal cord signal on imaging argue against a cord lesion

3. Hypokalemic/toxic periodic paralysis - normal electrolytes and metabolic panel exclude this, and reflexes are typically preserved in periodic paralysis, unlike this patient's global areflexia

- Global areflexia with ascending symmetric weakness from Neurological Examination — hallmark of acute polyradiculoneuropathy rather than myelopathy or functional weakness

- Albuminocytologic dissociation (protein 88 mg/dL, WBC 2/mm3) from CSF analysis — classic supportive finding excluding infectious polyradiculopathy
- Antecedent pneumonia three weeks prior from history — typical triggering infection for AIDP

### Recommendations
1. Initiate IVIG (0.4 g/kg/day x 5 days) or plasma exchange as first-line disease-modifying therapy, per equivalent efficacy data
2. Continue serial FVC/respiratory monitoring given the borderline FVC of ~70% predicted
3. Admit for continuous cardiac telemetry to monitor for autonomic instability

### Red Flags / Alerts
- Do not use corticosteroid monotherapy — it has not been shown to hasten recovery in AIDP and is not a substitute for IVIG or plasma exchange
- Do not defer respiratory monitoring — FVC can decline rapidly and mandates ICU transfer/intubation if it falls below 20 mL/kg or VC <40% predicted
