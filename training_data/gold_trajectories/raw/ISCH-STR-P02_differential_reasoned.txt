- Do not administer standard-dose IV tPA in confirmed/suspected moyamoya without explicit stroke-team and neurosurgical discussion of hemorrhagic risk
- Avoid hyperventilation and aggressive blood pressure lowering — both reduce perfusion through fragile moyamoya collaterals and can precipitate watershed infarction
- Do not attribute the recurrent stereotyped spells to anxiety or migraine going forward — they represent hemodynamic TIAs requiring vascular surveillance
