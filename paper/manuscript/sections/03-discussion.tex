\content
\begin{itemize}
\item The finding in plain words, no numbers repeated.
\item General ML lesson: an agent learns what its reward can see. An outcome reward pays for leaving
tests out whenever the answer can be reached without them; an executable process specification is a
verifiable reward that sees the work-up. Stated for one domain and one trained model.
\item Clinical lesson: small local models become viable on the right signal; physicians audit
policies, not weights. No claim about safety: unsafe actions were rare before and after training.
\item Limitations stated plainly; outlook.
\end{itemize}
