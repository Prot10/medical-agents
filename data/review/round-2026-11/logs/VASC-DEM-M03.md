## Changes
The diagnosis (cardioembolic multi-infarct vascular dementia from an LV apical aneurysm with thrombus) is unchanged. Management now follows the 2022 AHA LV thrombus statement: warfarin (INR 2-3) or a DOAC as a reasonable alternative, for at least 3 months, with repeat cardiac imaging at about 3 months to guide duration, and heart-failure referral for EF 38%. Aspirin is reviewed with cardiology rather than stopped by rule (anticoagulant monotherapy is usual with an old infarct and no recent coronary syndrome or stent); INR monitoring applies if warfarin is chosen. The prohibition on prolonged rhythm monitoring and its waste label are gone; the key evidence, the AF differential and the teaching point say monitoring is not needed for the initial decision but may matter if stopping anticoagulation is considered. The carotid criterion now accepts duplex or MR angiography (the stored neck MRA agrees with the duplex). The white matter red herring and the small-vessel differential say Fazekas 1-2 supports at most a minor small-vessel contribution. In the on-request battery, the 9th and 14th percentile scores are called low average. Ordering CT perfusion, coronary CTA or catheter cerebral angiography is now labelled harm (iodinated contrast allergy; invasive), and SPECT, levodopa challenge, DTI, perfusion MRI, fMRI and TEE are labelled waste.
