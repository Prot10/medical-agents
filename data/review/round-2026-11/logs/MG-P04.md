## Changes
MG-P04 (48-year-old man, LRP4-positive double-seronegative generalized MG, previously labelled functional). Serology: the extended antibody result is now two separate reports, so an LRP4 order returns only LRP4 (positive, 1:400) and a clustered AChR cell-based order returns only the live cell-based assay (clustered AChR and clustered MuSK negative); LRP4 stays required and the cell-based assay is a new recommended step. The reviewer's request to accept clustered AChR as an equivalent of LRP4 was not applied, because after the split that order no longer reveals the LRP4 result. Respiratory function is now required (inpatient with bulbar weakness and sleep apnoea) and in the stop rule, with ABN escalation criteria; a baseline ECG (sinus rhythm 74/min) is a new recommended step before pyridostigmine; thymic imaging also accepts contrast mediastinal MRI, and its rationale notes the iodinated contrast caution. Polysomnography and the speech and language battery are labelled waste. Management: pyridostigmine with a baseline ECG and the 360 mg/day maximum; prednisone 5-10 mg titrated to 30 mg (ABN outpatient protocol); the "above 20 mg/day" and "no beta-blocker" prohibitions are removed (the drug-advice must now says beta-blockers are used with caution and adds iodinated contrast); screening before immunosuppression with steroid monitoring; a speech and language therapy swallowing assessment; MGFA class and baseline MG-ADL recorded; the thymectomy statement now follows ABN and ICG 2020.
