## Changes
A 12-lead ECG is now a required step for the blackout in the second attack (NICE CG109) and must be done before the assessment; it returns a normal ECG (sinus rhythm, normal intervals, no pre-excitation, Brugada pattern or conduction disease). The structured history now records the colleague's witness account (found slumped and unresponsive, no jerking, tongue biting or incontinence, gradual recovery over 15-20 minutes). The diagnosis label is 'Sporadic hemiplegic migraine (ICHD-3 1.2.3.2): two attacks of gradual motor and sensory aura followed by headache, with decreased consciousness in the second attack', without 'all secondary causes excluded' (label change, accepted[0] only; synonyms unchanged), and the key evidence, seizure and cardiac-syncope differentials and the blackout red herring attribute the loss of consciousness to migraine only once the witness account and ECG give no other cause. Brain MRI is required and in the stop rule (verification pass 4) and MRA stays recommended, which is credited and never penalised by the stop rule; EEG or prolonged cardiac monitoring are not listed and fall within the one extra action allowed. The categorical prohibition of antithrombotics for presumed TIA was replaced by a conditional must: the decision rests on the clinical features of aura, the MRI excludes an infarct but not TIA, and the TIA pathway applies if a real suspicion of ischaemia remains. The literature step had already been removed (class LIT).
Verification pass 3: the structured-history report no longer cites a '1.2.3 criterion C' that the current ICHD-3 does not contain; it states the two criteria that apply (1.2 criterion C, 4 of 6 characteristics, and 1.2.3 criterion B, motor plus sensory aura, both fully reversible). No clinical conclusion changes.
Verification pass 4: brain MRI is now required before the assessment (ICHD-3 notes that sporadic hemiplegic migraine usually needs neuroimaging, and the antithrombotic decision rests on the MRI); a lumbar puncture is no longer penalised as waste, since ICHD-3 says it may be needed to exclude HaNDL, which is now in the differential (low).
