## Changes
The optional criterion that credited FDG-PET or perfusion SPECT is deleted, and perfusion SPECT is now labelled waste (it substitutes for FDG-PET only where FDG-PET is unavailable, NICE NG97); FDG-PET has no label (PCA, conditional test) but is no longer credited. Amyloid PET stays only as the second alternative of the optional amyloid criterion and earns nothing after the CSF. The stop-rule allowance is now 0: the required donepezil interaction check (fluoxetine) is a criterion and never counts, while uncredited tests after the required work-up are penalised. The follow-up list now shows the credited steps first, then amyloid PET, then studies served only if ordered (FDG, tau PET, SPECT, speech-language, performance status, visual fields). The difficulty text no longer mentions FDG or SPECT. The neuropsychological flags were already correct, and the 'overall abnormal = no' row is the MMSE 25/30, within its 24-30 range (display, CENTRAL).
Condition audit: the bedside screen scores (MMSE/MoCA totals, item losses, recall with cues, clock and copy results, fluency counts) were taken out of the neurological examination, where the agent saw them before ordering the cognitive assessment; they remain in the cognitive assessment report.
Condition audit: the head CT that answers an off-pathway CT order now shows the same atrophy and small vessel changes as the MRI instead of an empty normal report; the on-request tau PET report no longer carries an SUVR copied across cases.
