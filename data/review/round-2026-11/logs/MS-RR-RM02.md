## Changes
Reviewer 1's request is applied: MRI before LP is no longer enough on its own. The MRI-before-LP constraint is hard with a reason naming the herniation checks, and a new must (required_recommendations[1]) asks the agent to document no midline shift, no basal cistern or fourth-ventricle effacement, no papilloedema and stable consciousness before the LP; both MRI copies now state the basal cisterns and fourth ventricle patent and no tonsillar descent. Following the condition plan, the steroid must and prohibition no longer treat negative CSF cytology and flow cytometry as excluding lymphoma: the decision is joint with neurosurgery and neuro-oncology after CSF, MR spectroscopy is one input, and a stereotactic biopsy comes before steroids if neoplastic suspicion remains unless deterioration makes waiting unsafe. A biopsy order is now answered by a case report of the dominant lesion showing active demyelination (unscored), instead of the library's non-diagnostic text. The right face and arm episode 15 months ago is a possible earlier attack throughout and the diagnosis must asks for MS (not RR). The pre-DMT must is drug-specific. Perfect-agent check passes.
