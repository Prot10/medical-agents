## Changes

The HSV differential states the real LP interval (about 10 days after the onset of neuropsychiatric symptoms) instead of 'more than 72 hours'. In the drug-interaction output, the cephalosporin-hives warning now adds that a carbapenem such as meropenem may be given, as cross-reactivity with cephalosporins is low.
