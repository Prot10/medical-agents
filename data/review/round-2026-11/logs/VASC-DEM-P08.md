## Changes
Multi-infarct vascular dementia diagnosed on CT is unchanged, but the label no longer presents the carotid stenosis as symptomatic and the cause: left carotid atherosclerosis and device-detected paroxysmal atrial fibrillation are two coexisting potential mechanisms whose share in each infarct cannot be assigned, and the must, key evidence, red herring and cardioembolism differential say so. Anticoagulation is no longer automatic: the must asks for review of the stored atrial electrograms (the device report now states they are available) and, if AF is confirmed, a shared risk-based decision in which a DOAC is reasonable for episodes under 24 hours but not mandatory, with aspirin stopping if a DOAC starts (ESC 2024). MRI is no longer described as absolutely contraindicated: it is not needed and could proceed only after device identification and a specialist MR safety assessment under a dedicated protocol (HRS 2017); the label stays 'harm' because that pathway cannot be ordered, and CT remains the required imaging. The levodopa challenge, EP study, every MR-based study and the chest, mediastinal and chest X-ray studies are now labelled (waste, or harm for the EP study and MR), the stored reports are kept, and the reasonable on-request studies are listed first. Naming (10th), SDMT (11th) and free recall (20th) are low average under the AACN 2020 labels. Stop rule unchanged.
