## Changes
FND-P08 (24-year-old woman with type 1 diabetes on an insulin pump, sudden left arm weakness 3 hours after an insulin bolus, within the thrombolysis window). The capillary glucose and emergency brain imaging (MRI with DWI, or non-contrast CT) are now required steps and in the stop rule with the examination, glucose first. The acute-stroke must no longer says that thrombolysis is not indicated: it asks for glucose before any reperfusion decision, emergency imaging, and a stroke-team decision on thrombolysis from the deficit, the imaging, the time from onset and eligibility, and it states that a normal early diffusion-weighted MRI does not exclude ischemic stroke and that positive functional signs alone do not decide it. The stroke differential and the echocardiography and monitoring waste rationales were aligned with that. CT angiography and ECG stay optional. The performance-status follow-up moved after neuropsychology. Diagnosis label, examination and the stored results are unchanged; no literature step remains.
