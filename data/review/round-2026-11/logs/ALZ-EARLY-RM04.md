## Changes
Biomarker pathway: the optional FDG-PET criterion is deleted and FDG-PET and perfusion SPECT orders are now labelled waste (amnestic MCI with supportive MRI and conclusive CSF; SPECT only substitutes for unavailable FDG-PET, NICE NG97). Amyloid PET stays only as the second alternative of the single optional amyloid criterion, so after the CSF it earns nothing, and the stop-rule allowance is now 0, so any uncredited test after the required work-up is penalised. All stored reports (amyloid PET, FDG, tau PET, SPECT, speech-language) remain as answers if ordered and sit after the credited steps. The initial labs now state the clinical significance of the microcytic anaemia, iron indices and the raised TSH on levothyroxine. The reassessment must no longer says no medicine is approved for MCI: it says cholinesterase inhibitors and memantine are not recommended, and anti-amyloid antibodies require specialist eligibility assessment. The FTD differential and the difficulty text no longer mention FDG-PET. The neuropsychological flags were already correct (display issue, CENTRAL).
Condition audit: the bedside screen scores (MMSE/MoCA totals, item losses, recall with cues, clock and copy results, fluency counts) were taken out of the neurological examination, where the agent saw them before ordering the cognitive assessment; they remain in the cognitive assessment report.
