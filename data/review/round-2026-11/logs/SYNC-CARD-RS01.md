## Changes
The device must is now individualised: pace this admission without waiting for genetic results, and favour an ICD with pacing if a pathogenic LMNA variant is confirmed or the arrhythmic risk is high. The family history is described as suggesting, not establishing, an inherited conduction disease, throughout the differential, key evidence, avoided rationales and metadata. Tilt and EEG were already labelled as waste. Exercise testing, previously waste, and the exercise echocardiogram are now labelled harmful: unpaced complete AV block that caused a pulseless arrest today is an uncontrolled symptomatic arrhythmia, an absolute contraindication to exercise testing (Fletcher 2013). The bubble study, electrophysiology study and TEE are newly labelled as waste. Stored reports were not removed; they are answers if ordered. Driving advice covers both possible devices (ESC 2018 Practical Instructions).
