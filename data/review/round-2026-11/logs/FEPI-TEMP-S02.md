## Changes

The diagnosis now reads 'Focal epilepsy of right temporal lobe origin due to a right mesial temporal low-grade glioneuronal tumor (presumed DNET), with a focal-to-bilateral tonic-clonic seizure'. Ganglioglioma stays a moderate differential, and the key evidence says only histology separates the two. The surgical must now asks for a multidisciplinary epilepsy-surgery evaluation aiming at complete (maximal safe) resection, which gives the histology. Standalone biopsy is reserved for cases where resection is unsafe or tissue would change management, and presurgical neuropsychology (verbal and nonverbal memory) and individualized language lateralization are included, since her left-handedness does not establish dominance. Standalone stereotactic and open biopsy are harmful avoided actions. The lamotrigine must now specifies the standard slow titration and a maintenance dose adjusted to response and levels with the pill. The valproate prohibition is jurisdiction-neutral. The pregnancy test now has its own recommended criterion and its own negative serum beta-hCG panel. The labs criterion's evidence lists only what that order returns. An engineering note was removed from metadata.
