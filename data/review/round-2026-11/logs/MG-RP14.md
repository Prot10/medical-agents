## Changes
MG-RP14 (44-year-old woman with obstetric antiphospholipid syndrome and seronegative generalized MG confirmed by RNS and SFEMG). The live cell-based assay for clustered AChR and clustered MuSK is now required before she is called seronegative, and LRP4 is a separate recommended test, never a substitute (ABN includes it; the Italian recommendations say it "may be tested"). The stored cell-based result was split so a cell-based order and an LRP4 order each receive only their own result. A plain AChR or MuSK antibody order now receives the radioimmunoassay rather than the cell-based panel. Respiratory function is now required (inpatient with dysphagia). Thymic imaging also accepts contrast mediastinal MRI, and a baseline ECG is a new recommended step. RNS and SFEMG stay required. The brain MRI with MR angiography is an optional step for the APS stroke question, and the speech and language battery is labelled waste. The SFEMG sequence reasons now follow ABN 2025. Management: repeat AChR and MuSK antibodies at about 6 months, with repeat neurophysiology at a specialised centre if she remains triple seronegative or borderline; pyridostigmine before meals after a baseline ECG, maximum 360 mg/day; prednisone started in hospital at 5-10 mg and titrated to 30 mg, with azathioprine (after TPMT and NUDT15) as the steroid-sparing agent; screening before immunosuppression, including HPV vaccination; plasma exchange preferred for rescue in APS, with IVIG by ideal body weight (about 57 g) if used; thymectomy not routinely indicated without thymoma; MGFA class IIb and a baseline MG-ADL. The prohibition now forbids starting steroids as an outpatient while bulbar weakness is significant.
