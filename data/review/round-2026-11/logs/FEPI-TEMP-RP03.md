## Changes
Diagnosis: the label is now 'Focal epilepsy of left temporal lobe origin secondary to left temporal melanoma metastasis'; suspected checkpoint-inhibitor neurotoxicity is no longer part of it and stays a conditional differential (unlikely with normal CSF cells and negative antibodies, reconsidered if symptoms progress or fail to improve). Semiology: the hand automatisms, the slow head turn and the stare are now to the left, ipsilateral to the left temporal focus, in the HPI, examination, EEG reports, criterion and key evidence. Work-up: a recommended criterion now orders the prolonged video-EEG that captured nine habitual left temporal seizures (not in the stop rule). Key evidence: primary ASM prophylaxis in a seizure-free patient with brain metastases is not recommended (SNO/EANO 2021); treatment is indicated now that she has seizures. Levetiracetam 5.9 is described as below the laboratory range with missed doses and no established therapeutic range (also in the metadata difficulty text, which no longer says "subtherapeutic"); TSH 6.8 reads 'repeat with free T4'. Musts: continuing, holding or modifying ipilimumab and nivolumab is decided with oncology after multidisciplinary review of progression versus pseudoprogression and immune-related adverse events. Prohibition: high-dose steroids for presumed checkpoint-inhibitor encephalitis are prohibited only while the CSF is non-inflammatory and the metastasis explains the seizures. Biopsy is deliberately not labelled harmful here (tissue may be needed at MDT). Pathway order revised. Staging reports (body CT, whole-body PET, abdominal MRI) now include the known L2 and L4 vertebral metastases and the right L3-L4 foraminal tumor. Differential: pseudoprogression is unlikely but left open for the multidisciplinary review rather than settled as progression. Earlier this round: literature searches removed (LIT), MRI split into plain and gadolinium studies (MRI-CONTRAST). Not approved before this round.
