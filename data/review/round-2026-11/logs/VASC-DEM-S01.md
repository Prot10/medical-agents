## Changes
The diagnosis is unchanged. The key evidence no longer says that Fazekas 3 change with five lacunes is by itself sufficient: vascular attribution rests on the concordance of the two stepwise declines after probable lacunar events, the dysexecutive retrieval profile, the subcortical gait and the imaging (VasCog-2-WSO 2025). The teaching point, the MRI criterion rationale and the mixed-dementia differential follow the same rule. Timed gait testing is recommended, not required, and is out of the stop rule. The ECG is now required and in the stop rule because the two events were never investigated, and the antiplatelet must applies once the ECG shows no atrial fibrillation. Stop rule: screen, labs, MRI, ECG. Battery scores at the 12th and 20th percentile are labelled low average (AACN 2020). Two over-categorical sentences are softened: typical amnestic AD has a storage pattern, and lobar microbleeds would have suggested CAA. Ordering perfusion SPECT, a levodopa challenge, TEE or DTI is labelled waste; the stored reports are kept.
