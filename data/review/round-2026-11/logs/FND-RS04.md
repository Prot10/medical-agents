## Changes
The literature step is gone (class LIT). Brain MRI without contrast, with diffusion, is now required and part of the stop rule: a new right leg deficit of 5 days is imaged whatever the examination shows, because positive functional signs do not exclude a coexisting infarct or demyelinating lesion (Espay 2018; AHA/ASA 2026); the stored normal non-contrast MRI with DWI/ADC is unchanged. Video-EEG is now recommended rather than optional, because two typical events were witnessed (ILAE 2013; AAN 2025). The key evidence and metadata say the same. The soft constraint "examination before MRI" stays, and the on-request performance status is listed after the neuropsychology answer. No ECG: she is aware during the events.
