## Changes
Imaging before lumbar puncture: the hard rule is now 'brain imaging (CT or MRI) before the LP' rather than 'MRI before the LP', through a new optional criterion that either a CT or an MRI satisfies; a CT-first and an MRI-first trace both score full sequence credit and an LP before any imaging still caps the score. The MRI criterion (with gadolinium) stays required. Labs: CRP, WBC and neutrophils read 'Mild nonspecific inflammatory response'; glucose and HbA1c are attributed to dexamethasone and his known diabetes. Avoided actions: standalone stereotactic and open temporal biopsy (the CSF HSV-1 PCR is diagnostic) and resection (acute symptomatic seizures) are scored as harmful. Treatment musts: acyclovir already started on suspicion without waiting for MRI, LP or PCR; the status epilepticus must now also says to treat the recurrent seizures promptly, without waiting for MRI, LP, PCR or the continuous EEG, with continuous EEG to confirm and guide treatment. The stop rule already requires continuous ICU EEG. CT: its impression now records the mild local sulcal effacement, with no midline shift or basal cistern effacement. Earlier this round: literature searches removed (LIT), MRI split into plain and gadolinium studies (MRI-CONTRAST). Not approved before this round.
Sequence (verification pass 4): the soft rule that put the interaction check of acyclovir and the antiseizure medication after the MRI is removed, so preparing emergency treatment before imaging is no longer penalized; brain imaging (CT or MRI) before the lumbar puncture stays a hard rule.
