## Changes
MG-RP12 (74-year-old man with Parkinson disease and a resected lung adenocarcinoma, new bulbar-predominant AChR-positive MG with aspiration). Respiratory function is now required before the assessment, because severe bulbar weakness with aspiration is an ABN criterion for inpatient care. RNS with needle EMG stays required for the bulbar-onset ALS question. Thymic imaging now also accepts contrast mediastinal MRI, and a baseline ECG is a new recommended step before pyridostigmine. A levodopa challenge is a new optional step: it shows that the limb signs respond while the bulbar signs do not. Brain MRI (plain and contrast), the timed gait battery, the speech and language battery, the cognitive screen, the neuropsychological battery, DaTscan, cardiac MIBG, the smell test and whole-body FDG-PET are labelled waste. The SFEMG sequence reasons now follow ABN 2025. Management: IVIG 1 g/kg over 2-3 days, dosed on his actual weight of 66 kg because it is below his ideal body weight (about 66 g), after hepatitis serology and IgG, or plasma exchange, instead of 2 g/kg; pyridostigmine before meals after a baseline ECG, maximum 360 mg/day; prednisolone started in hospital at 5-10 mg and titrated to 30 mg, with early azathioprine (after TPMT and NUDT15 testing) because of his age; a screening and steroid-monitoring must; SLT swallowing assessment; MGFA class IVb and a baseline MG-ADL recorded. The prohibition now forbids starting steroids as an outpatient while bulbar weakness is significant, and the misattribution prohibition speaks of fatigable bulbar weakness, not of ptosis or diplopia, which he does not have.
