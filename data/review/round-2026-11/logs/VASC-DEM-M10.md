## Changes
Multi-infarct vascular dementia stays the diagnosis, now with "two coexisting potential stroke mechanisms" (atrial fibrillation and the severe symptomatic right carotid stenosis) rather than infarcts assigned to one mechanism each: the right PCA infarct lies outside the carotid territory and supports an additional embolic source, while the right MCA and border-zone infarcts may be haemodynamic, artery-to-artery or cardioembolic. The key evidence, differential, red herring and teaching points no longer say that atrial fibrillation cannot explain a border-zone lesion. Endarterectomy is no longer automatic: the must is an urgent vascular-team assessment of revascularisation eligibility, given that the last ipsilateral event was 5 months ago, weighing disability, life expectancy, operative risk and optimised medical therapy. A DOAC is started (apixaban 5 mg twice daily) and the antiplatelet plan is agreed with vascular surgery instead of stopping aspirin automatically. The blood pressure must no longer sets a fixed systolic floor of 120 mmHg: it avoids symptomatic and orthostatic hypotension until the stenosis is treated. The RAVLT delayed recall at the 20th percentile is low average (AACN 2020). Ordering perfusion SPECT, a levodopa challenge, cardiac MRI, DTI, CT or MR perfusion or a TEE is labelled waste and an EP study harm; the optional echo criterion now names TTE so it cannot be met by a TEE. Checks: perfect_agent.py passes; test_case_guards passes for this case. No MRI edited.
Verification (pass 1): the aspirin prohibition now allows an aspirin plan agreed with vascular surgery for a separate indication, as the must does.
