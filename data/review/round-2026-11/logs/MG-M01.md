## Changes
With positive AChR antibodies the diagnosis is serological, so repetitive nerve stimulation is now recommended rather than required and no longer gates the assessment; the stop rule requires AChR antibodies with thyroid function, contrast thymic imaging and respiratory function, which became required because of his dysphagia with coughing at meals and exertional dyspnoea. A baseline 12-lead ECG (sinus 72/min, normal intervals) is stored and recommended before pyridostigmine. The SFEMG stays optional and its report now follows the core results; both constraint reasons say what ABN 2025 says (serology first; RNS first, SFEMG where expertise is available when RNS is normal). The managements now follow ABN 2025: pyridostigmine 30 mg three to four times daily after an ECG, up to 360 mg/day; thymectomy for the thymoma with thoracic oncology referral, MG optimised first and IVIG 1 g/kg by ideal body weight (about 73 g) after hepatitis serology and IgG, or plasma exchange, only on clinical indication; prednisone started in hospital at 5-10 mg and raised by 5 mg on alternate days to 30 mg (the unsourced '20 mg/day' prohibition is replaced by 'no outpatient start with significant bulbar weakness', Evoli 2019); screening before immunosuppression and steroid monitoring; FVC with MIP and MEP twice daily with the ABN ICU/HDU criteria; an SLT swallowing assessment; MGFA IIb and a baseline MG-ADL. The levodopa challenge, the timed gait battery, the speech and language battery and whole-body FDG-PET are labelled waste. The drug-check report gives the 360 mg/day maximum, and the case's NCS/EMG fallback now agrees with the unstable deltoid motor units of the RNS report.
