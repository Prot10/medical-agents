## Changes
This case was replaced, as the physician asked, because it duplicated the right leg weakness and shaking events of S01 (and the other S cases). The new case (test split) is a different FND phenotype, labelled "Functional neurological disorder — functional gait disorder" (F44.4). Differential low: cervical myelopathy, cerebellar ataxia, primary orthostatic tremor, myopathy. Musts: M-DX (functional gait disorder), M-COMM, M-PHYSIO with gait retraining, M-PSY (generalized anxiety disorder, returned with the new job); prohibitions P1, P2, P3, P7. There is no literature step, no video-EEG and no ECG criterion (no loss of consciousness), and the stop rule is the examination with max_additional_actions 0.
