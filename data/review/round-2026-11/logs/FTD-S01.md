## Changes
The only clinical change is how alcohol is handled. bvFTD remains the primary diagnosis, but the escalating drinking (1-2 to 6-8 beers nightly, which he denies) is now treated as a possible contributor that is not excluded: must 1 asks the agent to explain why alcohol does not account for the whole progressive syndrome, to screen for dependence (AUDIT, SADQ) and past withdrawal, to arrange medically assisted withdrawal with alcohol services if he is dependent instead of abrupt unsupervised abstinence (his wife controls access as support, not as withdrawal management), and to give thiamine (NICE CG115, CG100). The alcohol differential, its red herring and key evidence say a contribution is not excluded, and answers naming alcohol-related brain damage as a contributor alongside bvFTD are no longer vetoed. The head CT waste rationale now says CT is the alternative when MRI is unavailable or contraindicated and is redundant here because the MRI is done (NICE NG97, SNLG/ISS 2024). The performance-status report was rewritten from his own history (Karnofsky 60, ECOG 2: needs physical guidance to shower, cannot work or manage money). Abnormal glucose, CDT and HbA1c rows now carry a one-line interpretation. Literature items were already removed by class LIT.
