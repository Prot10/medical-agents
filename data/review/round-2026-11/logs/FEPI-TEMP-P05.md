## Changes

The ED prolactin (48 ng/mL at 22 minutes) is now presented as at most supportive, outside the 10-20 minute window, and not able to separate seizure from syncope or exclude PNES, in its lab row, the key evidence and the PNES differential; the labs criterion no longer cites it. Cardiac syncope is now "highly unlikely", not excluded, with an intermittent arrhythmia reconsidered if events change. Video-EEG is now recommended and the repeat ECG optional; the stop rule is the epilepsy-protocol MRI and the routine EEG, and the cardiac-syncope prohibition now rests on those two studies. Stereotactic and open hippocampal biopsy and resection are avoided actions of severity harm. The lab interpretation was split into one sentence per panel so each order gets a clean sentence.
