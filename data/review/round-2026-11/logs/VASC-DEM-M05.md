## Changes
The diagnosis (mixed Alzheimer and vascular dementia after a left thalamic lacunar stroke) is unchanged. An amyloid biomarker is no longer required: CSF or amyloid PET is recommended and outside the stop rule (now screen, labs, MRI), because the two years of pre-stroke amnestic decline, the storage-type amnesia and Scheltens 3 atrophy already strongly support the Alzheimer component; the musts, key evidence, red herrings and differential now say a biomarker "if obtained" adds certainty, and donepezil is offered without a biomarker condition (NICE NG97). Impaired recognition is described as strongly supporting an amnestic neurodegenerative process but not disease-specific, and the VPL thalamic lacune as making an isolated strategic amnesia unlikely while stroke-related network effects may add to the decline. "Stop gliclazide" is replaced by an individualised hypoglycaemia-risk review with replacement by a lower-risk agent if needed (ADA 2026). The optional carotid criterion accepts duplex or MR angiography. Boston Naming 20th percentile is now low average. SPECT, tau PET, levodopa challenge, SSEP and DTI are labelled waste and catheter angiography harm.
