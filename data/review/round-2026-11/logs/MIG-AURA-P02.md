## Changes
The loop recorder is no longer required: its criterion, stop-rule entry and sequence constraint were removed, and ordering an implantable loop recorder is now an avoided action (waste) because AF is already documented on the GP Holter and the anticoagulation decision rests on CHA2DS2-VA, not on symptom-rhythm correlation; the stored ILR report is kept for an agent who orders it. Brain MRI moved from recommended to required, since silent embolic infarcts would make the episodes ischaemic and raise the score to 2; the stop rule is now assessment, MRI and drug check. The apixaban must is conditional: review with cardiology after the neurological reassessment, with no anticoagulation if the episodes are accepted as aura (CHA2DS2-VA 0, ESC 2024) and continued anticoagulation if a TIA cannot be confidently excluded (score 2). The prohibition built on the loop-recorder sinus rhythm was deleted, and the diagnosis now reads "Migraine with aphasic aura (ICHD-3 1.2.1) co-existing with paroxysmal atrial fibrillation (alcohol-related)" (label change, same codes and required components). The explanation must, the cardioembolic-TIA differential, key evidence items 1 and 3, the AF red herring and the metadata texts now say the clinical features and an MRI without infarct favour aura and that sinus rhythm during an episode does not exclude cardioembolism. The NSAID line in the acute-treatment must now applies only if apixaban is stopped. Literature step removed earlier by class LIT.
