## Changes
FND-RS02 is a new case and needs a full re-review. It replaces the former near-duplicate of RP03 with a record-derived case of functional visual loss (Pozuelo Moyano et al., J Med Case Rep 2023;17:340, PMC10416532, CC BY 4.0). A 42-year-old former accountant with chronic left facial pain since 31, treated with glycerol rhizolysis, microvascular decompression, radiosurgery and facial stimulation electrodes, has reported no light perception in either eye since 39 and is admitted for a disability-benefit review. On examination she has brisk pupils, optokinetic nystagmus and eye movements that follow a moving mirror, normal fundi, and left corneal anesthesia with punctate keratitis. Required: the functional-signs examination and one non-contrast brain MRI (or non-contrast CT, given her implanted leads) to exclude cortical blindness, which spares the pupils; both are in the stop rule. Recommended: pattern VEP and OCT (both normal, as in the paper). Optional: perimetry (unreliable, no stimulus seen), medication review, neuropsychology. ECG, echo, monitoring, CSF, FDG-PET, EMG and SSEP are waste. Label: 'Functional neurological disorder with functional visual loss (bilateral functional blindness)', F44.6. Musts: positive diagnosis, explanation, psychological therapy with pain-service management, ophthalmology care of the neurotrophic keratitis, and documentation for the disability review without calling the symptoms feigned; prohibitions include diagnosing feigning without evidence of intentional production. Differential: cortical blindness, optic neuropathy or retinal disease, B12 optic neuropathy, factitious disorder or malingering (all low), and the coexisting left neurotrophic keratopathy.
