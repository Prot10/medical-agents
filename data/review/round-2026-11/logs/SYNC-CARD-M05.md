## Changes
Because sudden palpitation immediately followed by syncope is a major high-risk feature (ESC 2018 Table 6), immediate in-hospital ECG monitoring (telemetry) is now required and part of the stop rule, with a soft ECG-first constraint like the event monitor; the event monitor stays required. The outpatient must now reads "Discharge only after in-hospital ECG monitoring without recurrence, with expedited electrophysiology follow-up for ablation now that the tachycardia is documented, the ECG shows no pre-excitation and the heart is structurally normal". AVRT is no longer a rejected answer: the AVRT differential now states that orthodromic AVRT over a concealed pathway cannot be excluded before an EP study and that management is the same, while WPW stays rejected (no pre-excitation). The EP study remains optional. The follow-up order now puts the EP study before the drug checks and the off-pathway tilt and MRI reports.
