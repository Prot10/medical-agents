## Changes
The AV block is now named by the ACC/AHA/HRS 2018 definitions throughout: paroxysmal 2:1 and high-grade (advanced) AV block, never Mobitz II, in the telemetry report and its identical fallback, the diagnosis label ("Cardiac syncope due to paroxysmal 2:1 and high-grade AV block (infranodal) on a background of trifascicular conduction disease"), the must R0, the monitoring evidence, the key evidence and the avoided-action rationales; each telemetry pause now matches the sinus rate (5.8 s from four non-conducted P waves at 52 bpm, 4.1 s from three at 58 bpm, 3.6 s from two at 50 bpm; minimum 28 bpm during 2:1 conduction at 56 bpm). The exercise test is no longer labelled useless: because the syncope happened on the stairs, a monitored exercise test (or exercise echo) is a recommended step (ESC 2018 Class I), required to come after the transthoracic echo (hard constraint), and not part of the stop rule. The pacing must now quotes the ESC 2021 Class I indication, and the driving must cites the ESC 2018 Practical Instructions (Group 1 after 1 week, Group 2 once device function is confirmed, national rules apply). Electrophysiology study, bubble study, TEE, invasive coronary angiography, coronary CTA, nerve conduction studies and polysomnography are now labelled waste for this patient; their stored reports are kept so an order still gets a result consistent with the case. Follow-ups and criteria were reordered so the exercise test sits after monitoring and the off-pathway reports come last.
