## Changes
This case was replaced, as the physician asked, because it duplicated the right leg weakness and shaking events of S01 (and the other S cases). The new case (train split) is a different FND phenotype, labelled "Functional neurological disorder — functional fixed dystonia of the right hand" (F44.4). Examination (required: collapsing weakness of right finger extension and variable resistance to passive extension that increases with attempted movement, count 2; fixed at rest from onset, no sensory trick or task specificity, no color, temperature, sweating, swelling, trophic change or allodynia), drug check (recommended: sertraline started a week before onset, no dopamine receptor antagonist, sertraline with ibuprofen bleeding risk), brain MRI optional (stored normal non-contrast report), neuropsychology optional; waste: ECG, echo, Holter, CT, CSF, FDG-PET, EMG/NCS, SSEP, DaTscan. Differential low: CRPS possibly coexisting (Budapest criteria not met), primary focal hand dystonia, drug-induced dystonia, structural lesion. Musts: M-DX (functional fixed dystonia), M-COMM, M-PHYSIO with occupational therapy and no immobilization, M-PSY with trauma-focused therapy for PTSD; prohibitions P1, P2, P3, P7 and 'Immobilize the hand in a cast or splint for the fixed posture'. There is no literature step, no video-EEG and no ECG criterion (no loss of consciousness), and the stop rule is the examination with max_additional_actions 0.
