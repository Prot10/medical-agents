The taper must now withdraws carbamazepine and phenytoin one at a time under supervision once the events are documented, then tapers diazepam 10 mg twice daily slowly over months, starting with reductions of about 5 to 10% every 2 to 4 weeks adjusted to withdrawal symptoms, and watches for rising diazepam levels and sedation as the enzyme inducers are withdrawn (Joint benzodiazepine tapering guideline 2025); the drug-check output now states that carbamazepine and phenytoin induce CYP3A4 and CYP2C19, which clear diazepam. The literature step was removed earlier this round (class LIT).
