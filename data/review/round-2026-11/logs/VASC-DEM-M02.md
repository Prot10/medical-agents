## Changes
Vascular dementia of subcortical small-vessel type stays the diagnosis, but the case no longer claims that alcohol explains only the gait. The label, the main must, the key evidence, the alcohol red herring and the alcohol-related dementia differential now say that continued drinking (about 40 g daily after 25 years of 100-120 g) may contribute to the cognitive decline, while progression across a year of reduced intake makes alcohol unlikely as the sole cause. Wernicke-Korsakoff syndrome stays low: absent confabulation and normal mamillary bodies lower suspicion without excluding it, and the executive-predominant profile with preserved recognition makes it unlikely. The abstinence must is replaced by assessment for medically assisted (inpatient or closely supervised) withdrawal because of the previous withdrawal seizure and his age, with continued thiamine (NICE CG115). The glucose prohibition now says not to delay glucose for hypoglycaemia while waiting for thiamine. Timed gait testing and the neuropsychological battery are recommended, no longer required, and leave the stop rule (now screen, labs and MRI). Battery rows use the AACN labels (FAS 9th and delayed recall 17th are low average). Perfusion SPECT, levodopa challenge, SSEP, skin biopsy and DTI are labelled waste; EMG stays acceptable because of the neuropathy. Checks: perfect_agent.py passes; test_case_guards (M02) 8 passed. No MRI edited.
