## Changes
The literature steps are gone (class LIT). The case no longer says CT excludes shunt failure: the key evidence, the shunt-malfunction differential and the CT criterion now say a normal CT makes it less likely without excluding it, and a new must asks for comparison with prior imaging and neurosurgical review if headaches or cognitive change persist. Stereotactic and open hippocampal biopsy and resection are avoided actions of severity harm. The valproate prohibition no longer quotes the UK MHRA procedure, and the optional labs criterion no longer cites a toxicology result its order does not return. Perfect-agent check passes.
