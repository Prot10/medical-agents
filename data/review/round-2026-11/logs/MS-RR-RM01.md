## Changes
Reviewer 1's request is applied: lumbar puncture in this man with a 4.2 cm tumefactive lesion now has an explicit safety gate. The MRI-before-LP constraint is hard, with a reason naming midline shift, basal cistern and fourth-ventricle effacement and tonsillar descent, and a new must (required_recommendations[1]) asks the agent to document from the MRI and examination that there is no shift or effacement, no papilloedema and stable consciousness before the LP, and not to do it otherwise; both MRI copies now state the fourth ventricle and tonsils explicitly so the check can be made from the report. Following the condition plan, the steroid must and prohibition no longer treat a negative CSF cytology and flow cytometry as excluding lymphoma: corticosteroids are decided with neurosurgery and neuro-oncology after CSF, with biopsy before steroids if neoplastic suspicion remains unless deterioration makes waiting unsafe (EHA-ESMO 2024). The right-hand episode two years ago is a possible, not proven, earlier attack throughout, and the diagnosis must asks for MS (not RR) with DIS, DIT and positive CSF. The pre-DMT must is drug-specific. Perfect-agent check passes with the hard constraint.
