## Changes
MG-RP11 (68-year-old man, AChR-positive generalized MG, MGFA IIb, with a 4.4 cm thymoma and reduced respiratory muscle strength). Repetitive nerve stimulation is now recommended instead of required and no longer has to be done before the assessment, since AChR binding 12.4 nmol/L makes the diagnosis. Thymic imaging now also accepts contrast mediastinal MRI, which returns a stored MRI of the same thymoma, and a baseline ECG (sinus rhythm 72/min, normal intervals) is a new recommended step before pyridostigmine. Both SFEMG sequence reasons now follow ABN 2025, and the respiratory and thymic imaging rationales cite ABN and ICG 2020 instead of the GBS 20/30/40 rule and MGTX. Brain and orbit MRI, FDG-PET (cerebral or whole-body), a repeat chest radiograph and the speech and language battery are labelled waste. Management: thymectomy for the thymoma with referral to thoracic surgery and thoracic oncology, MG optimised first; pre-operative IVIG now 1 g/kg over 2-3 days by ideal body weight (about 71 g) after hepatitis serology and IgG, or plasma exchange, instead of 2 g/kg; pyridostigmine after a baseline ECG with the 360 mg/day maximum; inpatient care, because the single-breath count of 22 meets the ABN inpatient criterion, with ICU or HDU referral on the ABN FVC and clinical criteria (the MIP and MEP thresholds are gone); prednisone started in hospital and titrated from 5-10 mg; the prohibition now forbids starting steroids as an outpatient while bulbar weakness is significant; a screening and steroid-monitoring must; MGFA class IIb and a baseline MG-ADL recorded.
