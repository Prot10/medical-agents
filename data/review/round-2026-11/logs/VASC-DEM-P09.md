## Changes
The diagnosis is unchanged. The plan no longer presumes that the artery of Percheron infarct was a small perforator occlusion: an ECG is now required (it had never been done for this infarct) and must be done before the diagnosis is submitted. The antiplatelet must now applies only if the ECG and the aetiological work-up find no indication for anticoagulation, and a new must asks for echocardiography and prolonged rhythm monitoring if the ECG and vessel imaging show no cause. Echo and monitoring stay optional criteria, and their stored reports (normal TTE, 14 days of sinus rhythm) are unchanged. The key evidence and the MRI criterion rationale name both possible mechanisms and rest the vascular attribution on concordance. The neuropsychological battery is now recommended, not required, and is out of the stop rule (screen, labs, MRI, ECG); it characterises the storage amnesia and sets the occupational baseline. Percentile rows use the AACN reference string (no flags changed), the CT fallback no longer says "lacunar", and ordering perfusion SPECT is labelled waste.
