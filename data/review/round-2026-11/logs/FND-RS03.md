## Changes
The literature step is gone (class LIT). Video-EEG is now recommended rather than optional, because a typical event was witnessed on the ward (ILAE clinically established level; recording raises it to documented, AAN 2025). A new recommended 12-lead ECG criterion screens the events with apparent loss of awareness (NICE CG109), with a case-specific normal ECG (sinus 82/min, QTc 428 ms on escitalopram); it is recommended, not required, because the syncope admission 6 months ago had an unrevealing cardiac work-up. Key evidence, the syncope red herring and the convulsive-syncope differential now say that the ECG is reasonable and normal and that Holter and echocardiography need not be repeated. The stop rule stays the examination only. Criteria and follow-ups are reordered to the condition order (examination, ECG, MRI, video-EEG, drug check, neuropsychology, then performance status).
