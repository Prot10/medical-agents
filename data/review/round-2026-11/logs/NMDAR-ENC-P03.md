## Changes
The physician approved this case; it was changed only for condition-wide template defects that the same physician ruled wrong in the other cases, and for one data contradiction. Must 1 (restart acyclovir in place of valacyclovir) now adds empirical bacterial meningitis cover (ceftriaxone 2 g IV every 12 hours with vancomycin) until the CSF Gram stain and culture are negative and uses the Graus 2016 sentence on an early negative HSV PCR. Must 2 uses the Abboud 2021 doses, states that combined therapy is started for the autonomic instability with recurrent seizures, and prefers IVIG (agitation, dysautonomia) with sodium monitoring. A bridging must is new. The escalation must waits 2 to 4 weeks, gives the cyclophosphamide dose and adds fertility preservation before cyclophosphamide. The benzodiazepine must cites Lejuste 2016. In the case's fallback laboratory panel, which answers an order for other drug levels, levetiracetam now reads 18 mg/L instead of 'not detected', matching his medication and the initial labs. The scrotal-ultrasound must is unchanged (post-HSV disease is not tumour-associated, Armangue 2018).
Condition audit: the drug-interaction check no longer names the diagnosis in its warning against high-potency D2 antagonists; it now says the reactions are reported in autoimmune encephalitis, because that check can be called before any test and so revealed the answer.
