CSF or amyloid PET is recommended rather than required and is no longer needed before the diagnosis (stop rule: screen, labs, MRI); no field any more says that the biomarker decides the cholinesterase inhibitor, which is offered on the clinical diagnosis (NICE NG97). Ordering tau PET, a levodopa challenge, cardiac MRI or DTI is labelled waste and an electrophysiology study harm; the stored reports are kept.
