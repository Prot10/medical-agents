## Changes
Literature steps were removed by the class change (commit 393c547e). The automatisms are now in the left hand, on the side of the left temporal focus, everywhere they are described (complaint, history, examination, video-EEG, criterion evidence), and the key evidence says unilateral automatisms are usually ipsilateral but lateralize weakly, with localization resting on the concordant MRI and EEG. The surgical must is now conditional: early discussion with an epilepsy surgery center, formal presurgical referral if seizures continue on an appropriate first-line ASM. For possible raised intracranial pressure a recommended MR venography was added (stored result: bilateral distal transverse sinus narrowing without thrombosis), and the neuro-ophthalmology must now asks for an opening pressure by lumbar puncture if papilledema is confirmed and treatment of confirmed intracranial hypertension before any skull-base repair; the IIH differential and key evidence reflect the venogram. The skull-base CT describes a full-thickness defect of the greater sphenoid wing. Standalone stereotactic and open biopsies are avoided actions (harm). A recommended serum beta-hCG (stored negative) precedes the ASM choice, and the valproate prohibition uses the jurisdiction-neutral wording.
