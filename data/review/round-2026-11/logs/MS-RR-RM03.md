## Changes
Reviewer 1's point is applied: the pre-treatment must (required_recommendations[4]) no longer requires universal HBsAg, anti-HBc, HCV and VZV screening before dimethyl fumarate. It now asks for the checks the chosen drug needs: for dimethyl fumarate, a full blood count with lymphocyte count and liver tests, with renal function and urinalysis where the local label requires them; for an alternative, that drug's own checks (anti-CD20: HBV screening and immunoglobulins; S1P modulator: ECG, blood count, liver tests, VZV IgG if immunity is unconfirmed, fundus examination), and live vaccines at least 4 weeks before only a drug that precludes them. The head CT waste rationale now says 'Head CT'. Literature steps are gone and the MRI is a plain/gadolinium pair (earlier class changes). Nothing else changed; perfect-agent check passes.
