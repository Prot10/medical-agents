## Changes
Literature step and MRI contrast were already fixed by class changes (checked). Corticosteroids are no longer gated on a negative CSF cytology and flow cytometry: after CSF is taken they are decided with neurosurgery and neuro-oncology, given if demyelination is secure, and a stereotactic biopsy comes first if neoplastic suspicion remains (unless deterioration makes waiting unsafe); the prohibition says a negative cytology does not exclude lymphoma. MRI-before-LP is now a hard constraint and a new must asks to document no midline shift, no basal cistern or fourth-ventricle effacement, no papilloedema and stable consciousness before LP; both MRI reports now state that the basal cisterns and fourth ventricle are not effaced. The pre-DMT must is drug-specific (interferon beta: blood count, liver tests, thyroid; alternatives per label). A biopsy order now returns a stored demyelination report (not scored). Minor: AQP4/MOG and CSF rationales, 'head CT' wording, lab evidence no longer cites an unordered metabolic panel. Not approved before this round. Checks: perfect_agent -v ok; served-answer probe of every criterion; test_case_guards clean; case_io check names no failure for this case.
