## Changes
Only the final recommendations changed; the patient, the pathway and every report are as the physician saw them. Must 1 now says to give empirical bacterial meningitis cover (ceftriaxone 2 g IV every 12 hours with vancomycin) with acyclovir until the CSF cell count, Gram stain and culture exclude bacterial infection, and replaces the 72-hour clause with the Graus 2016 sentence on repeating an early negative HSV PCR. Must 2 carries the Abboud 2021 doses, states that combined first-line therapy is started because the presentation is ICU-level (GCS 12, recurrent seizures, autonomic instability), prefers IVIG for the agitation and dysautonomia with sodium monitoring, and names the germ-cell tumour as the factor that favours plasma exchange. Two musts are new: bridging therapy after the acute course (oral prednisone taper, or monthly IVIG or methylprednisolone) and daily sodium monitoring with slow correction of the sodium of 131 mEq/L. The escalation must now waits 2 to 4 weeks after first-line therapy, decides at the earlier end for this severe case and gives the cyclophosphamide dose (600 to 1000 mg/m² monthly). The benzodiazepine must cites Lejuste 2016. The orchiectomy must is unchanged.
Condition audit: the drug-interaction check no longer names the diagnosis in its warning against high-potency D2 antagonists; it now says the reactions are reported in autoimmune encephalitis, because that check can be called before any test and so revealed the answer.
