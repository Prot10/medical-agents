## Changes
The safety plan is now explicit about violence. A new must asks the agent to assess the risk of violence now (intent toward the neighbour or others, threats to his wife, access to other weapons or ammunition including firearms kept elsewhere, his wife's safety), to agree a written safety plan with his wife, to keep firearms and ammunition out of the home permanently, and to arrange urgent psychiatric or emergency assessment if the risk is imminent (Betz 2018); the capacity must keeps driving, finances and lasting power of attorney. The drinking (rare to 4-6 beers daily) is now treated as a contributor that is not excluded, with a new must for dependence screening (AUDIT, SADQ), medically assisted withdrawal if dependent rather than abrupt abstinence, and thiamine (NICE CG115, CG100); the differential and red herring say so and qualified answers naming alcohol as a contributor are no longer vetoed. CT stays the required structural study and MRI stays waste (claustrophobia, sedation declined). The performance-status report was rewritten from his history (Karnofsky 70, ECOG 1). Literature items were already removed by class LIT.
