## Changes
Vascular dementia after perioperative border-zone infarction stays the diagnosis, but the case no longer claims that symmetric watershed infarcts prove a purely haemodynamic mechanism: the label says "predominantly haemodynamic, with perioperative embolism not excluded", and the key evidence, red herring, cardioembolic differential (still low), main must, MRI rationale and teaching points say that documented prolonged hypotension strongly supports a haemodynamic contribution while watershed infarction can be embolic or mixed. Anticoagulation is still not started because no cardioembolic source requiring it has been found. The blood-pressure must no longer sets a floor of about 120 mmHg or an upward target: the current control (128/72 mmHg, within the usual target) is maintained, avoiding symptomatic and orthostatic hypotension. Furosemide is reviewed with cardiology (indication, volume status, orthostatic BP, renal function, electrolytes) and reduced or stopped if no indication remains, rather than stopped by rule. The neuropsychological battery is recommended, not required; the stop rule is screen, labs, MRI, ECG and TTE. RAVLT delayed recall 24th percentile is now low average. Perfusion SPECT, levodopa challenge, cardiac MRI, DTI, CT and MR perfusion and the chest studies are labelled waste.
