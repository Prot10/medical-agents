## Changes
Literature steps were removed by the class change (commit 393c547e). Topiramate is now immediate release 50 mg twice daily, and the history explains why the dose stayed at 100 mg/day (200 mg/day at age 20 caused word-finding difficulty and paresthesia); the switch must allows a cautious increase only with that intolerance in mind, with highly effective contraception and pregnancy-risk counselling. A recommended MR venography was added for postpartum cerebral venous thrombosis (stored result normal), and the CVT differential and the MRI wording no longer treat flow voids as excluding it. For late postpartum eclampsia, AST/ALT and urine protein/creatinine (normal) were added to the labs and the labs criterion, the differential calls it possible but unlikely, and a must asks for postpartum obstetric review with serial blood pressure. The low bicarbonate and topiramate rows are interpreted, with a must to recheck bicarbonate before any topiramate increase. A recommended serum beta-hCG (negative) was added, the valproate prohibition uses the jurisdiction-neutral wording, and standalone hippocampal biopsies and a resection are avoided actions (harm).
