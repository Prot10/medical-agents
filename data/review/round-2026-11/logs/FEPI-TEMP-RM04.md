## Changes
Diagnosis: the label now says the tumor is presumed ganglioglioma or DNET and gives no WHO grade, since both type and grade need histology; the red herring on the grandfather's tumor and the metadata texts match. Semiology: the hand automatisms are now in the left hand (ipsilateral to the left temporal focus); right head version, right arm clonic jerking and the post-ictal right pronator drift are unchanged. Labs: hemoglobin, MCV, ferritin and transferrin saturation now carry 'Microcytic anemia with low ferritin: iron deficiency; persistent despite oral iron'; the optional lab criterion now orders a blood count and iron studies with the glucose, sodium and calcium, so the anemia behind the gastrointestinal-evaluation must is actually returned. Musts: the surgical must now asks for multidisciplinary epilepsy-surgery evaluation aiming at complete (maximal safe) resection, which gives the histology, with a standalone biopsy only if resection is unsafe or tissue would change management; language lateralization and mapping still precede it. Prohibitions: valproate is prohibited as not first-line for focal epilepsy, without the UK MHRA procedure. Avoided actions: standalone stereotactic and open biopsy are scored as harmful because they do not change management (the histology comes from the indicated resection; a biopsy only if the lesion cannot be safely resected); resection is not labelled because surgery is the plan. Pathway: follow-ups reordered (gadolinium MRI, MR spectroscopy, video-EEG, interaction check, biopsies). Work-up: a recommended criterion now orders the gadolinium MRI, which shows no enhancement of the lesion (suspected tumour). Earlier this round: literature searches removed (LIT), MRI split into plain and gadolinium studies (MRI-CONTRAST). Not approved before this round.
