## Changes
Mixed Alzheimer and vascular dementia with both components severe stays the diagnosis. Formal neuropsychological testing and the amyloid biomarker (CSF or amyloid PET) are both recommended rather than required, and neither is needed before the diagnosis (stop rule: screen, labs, MRI). The case no longer says that the white matter burden is "sufficient" for a vascular dementia or that the storage amnesia "demands" the biomarker: the vascular attribution rests on concordance of the dysexecutive profile, the gait and the imaging, and the dense storage amnesia with impaired recognition and MTA grade 3 points to a coexisting Alzheimer process (impaired recognition is described as strongly supportive but not disease-specific). The main must names the mixed dementia on these clinical and MRI grounds, with a biomarker adding certainty if obtained, and the cholinesterase-inhibitor prohibition no longer requires biomarker confirmation. Boston Naming at the 14th percentile is low average (AACN 2020). Ordering perfusion SPECT, tau PET, a levodopa challenge or DTI is labelled waste; the stored reports are kept.
