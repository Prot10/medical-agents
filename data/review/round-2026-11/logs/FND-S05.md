## Changes
The literature step is gone (class LIT). Three tests are now required and in the stop rule, with the examination: a non-contrast brain MRI with DWI, because a new right leg deficit of 3 days is imaged whatever the examination shows (Espay 2018; AHA/ASA 2026); a 12-lead ECG, because the events include apparent loss of responsiveness (NICE CG109), now answered by a case-specific normal ECG (sinus 96/min matching the vitals, QTc 430 ms) instead of the shared template; and video-EEG, because no event has been seen by a clinician, so only a recorded habitual event without ictal change takes the diagnosis past the ILAE possible level (ILAE 2013; AAN 2025). The diagnosis must now names the recorded event. Criteria and follow-ups are reordered to the condition order. Patient text, reports, differential, other musts and prohibitions are unchanged.
