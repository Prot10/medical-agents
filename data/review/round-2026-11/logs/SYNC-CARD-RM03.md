## Changes
The case gained three recommended steps that a cardiologist would take in this post-infarct patient with LVEF 36-38% and syncope. The first is a coronary assessment, by invasive angiography or coronary CTA, which shows the chronic LAD occlusion; it satisfies the existing must for ischaemic evaluation, which now names either test. The second is an EP study with programmed ventricular stimulation. The stored EP report now records stimulation from the RV apex and outflow tract with no inducible sustained monomorphic VT, alongside the HV interval of 92 ms. The CRT-pacemaker must now gives that non-inducibility as part of its reason, and the VT differential cites it. The third is a monitored exercise test (exercise echo accepted), because the first syncope occurred on the stairs. It must follow the echocardiogram, and it replaces a previous waste label. The AV block wording now follows the 2018 ACC/AHA/HRS definitions: Mobitz II only for the documented 3:2 conduction, with 2:1 named as such, and the tilt-test pause called high-grade block. The bubble study and TEE are labelled waste. The driving must now cites the ESC 2018 Practical Instructions. The diagnosis, the other musts and the remaining reports are unchanged. The perfect-agent check passes; coronary CTA is answered by the case's own fallback CTA report.
