## Changes
The literature step is gone (class LIT). The examination is now joined in the stop rule by three required tests: a non-contrast brain MRI with DWI for the left-sided weakness of 24 hours (positive signs do not exclude a coexisting infarct; Espay 2018, AHA/ASA 2026); video-EEG, because no staring spell was seen by a clinician (ILAE 2013; AAN 2025); and a 12-lead ECG for the events with apparent loss of responsiveness (NICE CG109), answered by a case-specific normal ECG (sinus 88/min, QTc 426 ms) instead of the shared template. The motor examination now states the inconsistency: left arm 1-2/5 on formal testing, yet both arms are held outstretched against gravity, after which the left arm drifts down without pronation; the report records this as an inconsistency finding. The left hand tremor now entrains throughout (it takes up the rhythm she taps with the right hand); the contradictory "stops" wording is gone. The diagnosis must names the recorded event. Criteria and follow-ups are reordered to the condition order.
