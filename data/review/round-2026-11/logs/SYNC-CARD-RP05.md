Corticosteroids start once infection is reasonably excluded, with timing set by the multidisciplinary team, since the cultures are pending to 6 weeks. Driving advice gives the ICD interval (1 month for Group 1). The exercise test and exercise echocardiogram are labelled harmful (uncontrolled symptomatic arrhythmia), and the bubble study, electrophysiology study, TEE and mediastinal MRI as waste. Their stored reports remain only as answers if ordered, and the stop rule (3 extra actions) stays.
