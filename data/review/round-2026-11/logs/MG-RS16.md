## Changes
Respiratory function is now required (mild dysphagia and dysphonia, steroids planned) and must be done before the diagnosis is submitted; repetitive nerve stimulation with needle EMG stays required because the needle EMG separates MG from neck extensor myopathy and motor neuron disease in this head drop. The stored ECG (sinus 70/min, LVH by voltage, no ischaemia) is a recommended baseline before pyridostigmine, and the medication review is recommended because atorvastatin is a drug used with caution in MG. The brain and cervical spine MRI is an optional criterion for the myelopathy question, its report now listing the spondylosis without cord contact and the reduced nigral SWI signal behind its impression. The gait battery, levodopa challenge, speech and language battery, cervical spine CT and coronary CT angiography are labelled waste, and invasive coronary angiography is labelled harmful (stable asymptomatic coronary disease, iodinated contrast). SFEMG follows the core results, with constraint reasons that say what ABN 2025 says. Management: pyridostigmine after an ECG with a heart-rate watch and the 360 mg/day ceiling; prednisone from 5-10 mg, raised by 5 mg every 3-5 days to 30 mg (the unsourced 20 mg/day prohibition became 'do not start at full dose at once'), with early azathioprine after TPMT/NUDT15 because of diabetes, ischaemic heart disease and age (ABN 2025 rather than Sanders), close glucose control, screening before immunosuppression and steroid monitoring with bone densitometry; atorvastatin continued for his coronary disease and reviewed if weakness worsens; the collar and SLT swallowing assessment kept; MGFA IIIa with a baseline MG-ADL. The thymectomy statement now cites ABN 2025 and ICG 2020 rather than the MGTX enrolment range. A scored swallow assessment needs a new vocabulary term (CENTRAL).
