## Changes
This case was replaced, as the physician asked, because it duplicated the right leg weakness and shaking events of S01 (and the other S cases). The new case (train split) is a different FND phenotype, labelled "Functional neurological disorder — functional tremor of both arms" (F44.4). Examination (required: entrainment to a 3 Hz tapping rhythm, distractibility during a ballistic movement and serial 7s, count 2), TSH (recommended, stored normal TSH and free T4 as separate panels so a TSH order returns only TSH), sertraline review (recommended; the stored drug check names tremor as an SSRI adverse effect and finds no interaction), neuropsychology optional; waste: ECG, echo, Holter, CT, CSF, FDG-PET, EMG/NCS, SSEP, DaTscan and brain MRI {} (no focal sign). Differential low: enhanced physiological or SSRI-induced tremor, essential tremor, hyperthyroidism, Parkinson's disease. Musts: M-DX (functional tremor), M-COMM, M-PHYSIO with functional tremor retraining, M-PSY (generalized anxiety disorder and the anxiety and intrusive memories since the accident); prohibitions P1, P2, P3, P7. There is no literature step, no video-EEG and no ECG criterion (no loss of consciousness), and the stop rule is the examination with max_additional_actions 0.
