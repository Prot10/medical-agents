The copied rigidity and slow gait are gone; the drinking is the case's red herring and alcohol-related brain damage an explicit, low-likelihood mimic. Labs are the NICE NG97 set (glucose 128 in treated diabetes); syphilis and HIV serology answer a separate order. Genetics stays optional, follows the assessment and is written as a laboratory report; no must presumes its result (the musts are counselling before testing and follow-up for emerging motor neuron disease, plus the Rascovsky, alcohol, safety and caregiver musts). The literature search is optional, last and general; FDG-PET has an authored SPECT alternative; the CT and drug fallbacks were corrected or removed; the differential is the assessment-time view.
