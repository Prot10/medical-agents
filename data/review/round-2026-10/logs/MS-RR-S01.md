## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

A 28-year-old woman with day-3 painful left optic neuritis (20/80, RAPD) and a prior right arm and band-like trunk sensory attack 6 months ago. The fundus is now normal (no pallor at day 3) and the OCT shows normal, symmetric pRNFL and GCIPL in both eyes, as expected acutely; the VEP keeps the prolonged left P100 (128 ms). The brain MRI now reports only brain and orbit findings (periventricular, right frontal and left parietal juxtacortical, left middle cerebellar peduncle, an incomplete-ring enhancing right periventricular lesion, an enhancing swollen left intraorbital optic nerve) and states that the cord was not assessed; the cord lesions appear only on the spine MRI, where the T6-T7 lesion was moved to the left lateral column to match her left-sided pyramidal signs. The 3-month repeat MRI was deleted. The work-up now reads brain MRI, spine MRI, mimic labs (required), AQP4/MOG by cell-based assay (recommended), then CSF, VEP, OCT, drug check and literature (optional). CSF loses myelin basic protein and gains a kFLC index of 38.5 with the six OCB; the AQP4 and MOG rows are labelled as cell-based assays. Musts: the 2024 McDonald formulation (5 of 5 regions), methylprednisolone per NICE NG220 with ibuprofen stopped during the course, DMT by activity with pregnancy and contraception discussed, pre-DMT screening, vitamin D for the low level and Uhthoff counselling. The differential is updated to the results (NMOSD, MOGAD, sarcoidosis, B12, NPSLE, ADEM, Susac all low).
