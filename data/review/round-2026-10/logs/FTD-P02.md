A 48-year-old senior accounting partner with no psychiatric history has had 20 months of offensive remarks, a sexual proposition, about $850,000 of impulsive property contracts, compulsive household "rules", loss of interest and loss of empathy, with no insight. She was treated as a mood disorder and quetiapine had no effect. The patient block no longer summarises the syndrome or prints the MoCA and PHQ-9; these are returned by the assessment (5 of 6 Rascovsky features; MoCA 25/30; PHQ-9 4). The required pathway is: assessment, neuropsychology (executive and social-cognition impairment with relatively spared memory), the reversible-cause blood panel (normal apart from fasting glucose 102), and MRI (borderline right frontal sulcal prominence only). The autoimmune antibodies and RPR/HIV are no longer in the baseline labs; each is served if ordered. FDG-PET or perfusion SPECT is now recommended, because with an equivocal MRI it is the imaging support that probable bvFTD needs; it shows right-predominant frontal hypometabolism. Counselled genetic testing (GRN nonsense variant), plasma progranulin (low) and literature are optional. The must "mandates FDG-PET and genetic panel" and the drug-check output are deleted.
