The MMSE item losses add up to 20 and the "estimated" MoCA is gone. The meningioma has its own must (characterise, surveillance MRI, neurosurgery only if it grows or causes symptoms), a differential entry and a prohibition on attributing the decline to it. Depression drops to low in the differential, since the decline continued through 8 months of remission off sertraline, and restarting an antidepressant for cognition is prohibited while she is euthymic. PHQ-9 moves from the lab report to the clinical assessment. The CSF report drops NfL and the mechanistic text; amyloid status can come from CSF or a positive amyloid PET, and the DaTscan fallback is gone. The literature step is gone; the donepezil check is optional, limited to donepezil, and no longer recommends an ECG. Musts also cover disclosure with the interpreter, living alone safely, a cholinesterase inhibitor trial, and mood and activity.
