## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

The required work-up is now EMG/NCS, brain MRI, spine MRI (cervical, thoracic and lumbosacral) and mimic-exclusion bloods, then baseline respiratory function (recommended), the five-gene ALS panel after counselling (optional) and the riluzole interaction check (recommended); the literature step "neurosurgery consultation deferred" is gone and its content is a prohibition of lumbar surgery. The HPI no longer reports the lumbar MRI result or the reasoning about upper motor neuron signs; it says the GP found bilateral Babinski signs and brisk knee jerks and that neurosurgery asked neurology to assess before any surgical decision. The EMG keeps its findings (fibrillations and chronic change in leg and hand muscles, paraspinal fibrillations at L3-S1, T7-T9 and C5-C7, tongue fasciculations only), adds normal median and ulnar motor studies with no conduction block, and gives sural amplitudes (4 and 5 uV) that are actually reduced, in keeping with his diabetes and reduced toe vibration. The earlier teaching that lumbar paraspinal denervation cannot come from foraminal stenosis was wrong and has been removed; the deciding evidence is the denervation lumbar stenosis cannot reach (cervical, thoracic, hand, tongue) and the UMN signs. Brain and spine reports are now separate and region-pure. Labs are regrouped with liver tests and free T4 added (normal); CK 698 U/L is 4.0x ULN. FVC 79% and SNIP 62 cmH2O are reduced, so the musts refer him to the respiratory/NIV service now; other musts are diagnosis and disclosure, speech and swallowing assessment, riluzole with SmPC monitoring, falls prevention with ankle-foot orthoses, MDT and genetic counselling. The differential places lumbar stenosis and diabetic amyotrophy low and adds metastatic prostate cancer (very low).
