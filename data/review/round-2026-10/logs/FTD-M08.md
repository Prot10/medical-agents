## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

A 55-year-old social worker with HIV (Biktarvy 8 years, undetectable for 6), hypertension and previous depression, with 15 months of disinhibition, loss of empathy and rigid, stereotyped routines, on medical leave. The required work-up is now: informant-based assessment (3 Rascovsky features), neuropsychology (executive and social-cognition deficits with preserved recognition, construction, processing speed and motor function), the reversible-cause blood panel, plasma HIV viral load with CD4 (not detected; 680), MRI (bilateral frontal atrophy, normal white matter, no post-contrast sequence in a non-contrast protocol), and lumbar puncture for CSF HIV RNA (not detected) after the MRI. JCV PCR and cryptococcal antigen are optional because CD4 is high and the white matter normal; their results stay in the CSF report. The antiretroviral interaction review stays recommended and now gives correct Biktarvy content (no cobicistat; inducers contraindicated; cations; no interaction with sertraline or trazodone). Literature, FDG-PET/SPECT and genetics are optional. Musts: Rascovsky certainty; why HAND, CSF escape, OI and depression do not explain it; continue ART; safety including fitness to practise; support without ChEI/memantine; counselling before genetic testing. The HPI no longer summarises the syndrome or prints the CD4, and its timeline is consistent.
