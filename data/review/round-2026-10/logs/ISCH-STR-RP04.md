## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

A 43-year-old woman with migraine with aura had her usual zig-zag scotoma. About 20 minutes later, as it faded, anomic aphasia and right arm weakness began and persisted, with only a mild headache; she wanted to take her sumatriptan. The exam now agrees with the HPI on that sequence, the visual sign is recorded as extinction, and NIHSS 6 is itemised. The CT reads as a plain report (no haemorrhage, ASPECTS 10). The central change answers the reviewer: thrombolysis is decided at the bedside on the values available, not on a lupus anticoagulant result that cannot exist 50 minutes into a stroke code. She takes no anticoagulant, INR is 0.94 and platelets are 138, so the isolated aPTT of 46 s does not justify withholding IVT, and the case expects her to receive it. CTA shows no occlusion, so there is no thrombectomy. The antiphospholipid panel, now a required follow-up drawn before any anticoagulant, then explains the aPTT: triple positive, with persistence to be confirmed at 12 weeks. A normal echo (no Libman-Sacks lesion, no shunt) and 48 hours of sinus rhythm exclude the competing mechanisms. Sumatriptan is stopped permanently and ibuprofen stopped. Secondary prevention is aspirin after 24-hour imaging, then warfarin (INR 2 to 3) if APS is confirmed, and no DOAC. The literature step now cites real sources (Sydney criteria, EULAR 2019, TRAPS).

- aPTT and thrombolysis: AHA/ASA 2019 lists an aPTT above 40 s as an exclusion, but that item is framed around heparin exposure (COR III, C-EO). The case, following the reviewer, expects IVT because there is no anticoagulant exposure and the aPTT is isolated. Please confirm the physicians accept "give IVT" as the single correct decision rather than "IVT reasonable".
- Interim antithrombotic: the case gives aspirin after 24-hour imaging and warfarin once APS is confirmed at 12 weeks. Some clinicians start warfarin on a single triple-positive result. I chose aspirin first so that repeat lupus anticoagulant testing is not confounded, and noted this in key evidence [6]. A reviewer may prefer early warfarin.
- NIHSS item 3: I scored visual extinction as 1 on item 3 (NIH rule for extinction) as well as 1 on item 11. Some raters score item 3 as 0 when the fields are full to single stimuli. That reading gives NIHSS 5; the decision is unchanged.
