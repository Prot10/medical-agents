## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing
The work-up now reads as a reassessment of a presumed MMN: EMG/NCS, brain MRI, spine MRI and blood tests (including a repeat anti-GM1 IgM) are the required actions 1-4, CSF is optional for the inflammatory neuropathy question, followed by baseline respiratory function (recommended staging), an optional ALS gene panel and the riluzole check (recommended, last); the literature step is gone. The history now says the original EMG reported a possible partial conduction block, and the repeat EMG states that this block is not reproduced at standard or proximal sites, with active denervation in all four regions, chronic reinnervation in the hand muscles and normal sensory studies; the report no longer recommends pulmonary tests or calls the old study an artifact. The brain MRI describes only the brain (supportive corticospinal and precentral signal, no brainstem or other lesion); the spine MRI is normal. Labs are reorganised into standard panels with CK 842 U/L stated as 4.8x ULN and the repeat anti-GM1 IgM negative. FVC 74% and SNIP 58 cmH2O are reported as reduced, and the musts now refer him to the respiratory/NIV service. MMN is low in the differential, the target diagnosis and the LMN-predominant phenotype are no longer listed as competitors, and CIDP is added as very low. A must stops the monthly IVIG and a prohibition forbids continuing IVIG or other immunotherapy for ALS; the other musts follow the condition plan (disclosure with an explanation of the revised diagnosis, speech and swallowing assessment for tongue atrophy and palatal weakness, riluzole with its LFT schedule, MDT, genetic counselling).
