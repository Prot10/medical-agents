## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing
NMDAR-ENC-P02 (19-year-old man, all six Graus groups, ICU-level) now gives the CSF anti-GluN1 result once, in the CSF report (positive 1:128), and the serum result once, in the labs (positive 1:64). The labs no longer carry CSF values, the "Exclusion studies" text or duplicates. They now include free T4, explicit neuronal-surface and onconeural panels (Ma2 negative) and normal AFP, beta-hCG and LDH. The testicular ultrasound is written in radiology voice (1.4 cm solid-cystic mass with calcification, consistent with a germ-cell neoplasm). The staging CT is now a contrast chest-abdomen-pelvis body-imaging study with no metastasis, and it no longer names histology. Both the staging CT and the markers are recommended criteria. A 24-hour continuous EEG follow-up is consistent with the routine EEG (five right temporal seizures, persistent extreme delta brush) and is a recommended escalation. Literature and drug interactions are optional, with general, report-voice outputs. The musts are the canonical set plus urgent urological referral for radical inguinal orchiectomy, ICU admission, lorazepam for catatonia, early escalation and sperm cryopreservation before cyclophosphamide. The differential is the assessment-time view (all alternatives low). Fallback PET, specialized-test and Holter reports that contradicted the case were removed or rewritten.
