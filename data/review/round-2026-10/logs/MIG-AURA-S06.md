## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing
The HPI timeline now holds together: amitriptyline 10 mg was used about 18 months ago and stopped at conception, attacks were monthly and perimenstrual before pregnancy, occasional in pregnancy, and two a month since her periods returned 3 months after delivery and she went back to work. The structured ICHD-3 assessment is written for her (visual aura only, 20-25 min, 5 of 6 C characteristics, each SNNOOP10 flag stated, normal examination). The pathway is the assessment, then a drug check of sumatriptan, ibuprofen, amitriptyline and propranolol in lactation (LactMed: low milk transfer; ergots contraindicated; topiramate and valproate cautions), then an optional general literature search. Musts: migraine with typical aura without imaging; sumatriptan with ibuprofen or paracetamol at headache onset plus metoclopramide, without interrupting breastfeeding; a preventive discussion (amitriptyline 10 mg or propranolol) since two severe attacks a month reach the AHS 2021 "consider" threshold; contraception counselling (combined methods UKMEC 4 for the aura; progestogen-only methods UKMEC 2, copper IUD UKMEC 1); diary and review. Prohibitions: combined hormonal contraception, ergots while breastfeeding, valproate, interrupting breastfeeding for compatible drugs. Anaemia and "normal MRI" references, which had no data behind them, are gone.
