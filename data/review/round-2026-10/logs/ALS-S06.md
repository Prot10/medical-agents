## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing
The work-up now reads EMG/NCS, brain MRI, spine MRI and blood tests first, then respiratory function, the gene panel and the riluzole check; the literature step is gone. The EMG report states the motor conduction studies with no conduction block and attributes the mildly reduced sural responses to a length-dependent sensory neuropathy without citing the diabetes; its findings now support the impression's chronic change. The labs are reported as standard panels with a full blood count, metabolic panel and numeric thyroid tests (CK 820 U/L, 4.7x ULN; glucose 134 mg/dL in known diabetes). Respiratory function is reported as reduced (FVC 78%, SNIP 62 cmH2O) and drives an NIV-service referral now. The gene panel reports all five genes. The riluzole check states no interaction with metformin or lisinopril and the standard LFT schedule, without AMX0035. Musts were rebuilt as management (diagnosis and disclosure, respiratory referral, speech and swallowing assessment, riluzole with monitoring, MDT, genetic counselling); diabetic amyotrophy is low in the differential and the diabetic neuropathy is described as coexisting.
