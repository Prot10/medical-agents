## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing
GBS-RS16 is now a coherent cardiac ICU day 3 case. A 73-year-old woman on apixaban for atrial fibrillation had PCI for an inferior STEMI and started aspirin and ticagrelor. About 20 hours later she developed ascending flaccid areflexic weakness (MRC sum score 14/60) with bulbar weakness, and she was intubated on day 2. The patient block no longer gives the CSF, antibody or serology results. The bedside NCS shows a demyelinating polyradiculoneuropathy, and the CSF shows raised protein with 3 cells. The respiratory step reports the last values before intubation (FVC 16 mL/kg, MIP -20, MEP 28, a 38% fall), and telemetry covers only the first 24 hours. A normal platelet count lowers HIT to very_low. The raised procalcitonin and CRP, the heparin exposure and the timing after PCI are red herrings. Stroke, cord haematoma, sepsis and critical illness neuropathy are lowered with case reasons. The interaction check is required and comes before the lumbar puncture. Management is continued ventilation without succinylcholine and IVIg 2 g/kg now, with thrombotic precautions. The LP is agreed with cardiology, with DAPT continued and apixaban timed by the anti-Xa level. Telemetry continues, and metformin is held.
