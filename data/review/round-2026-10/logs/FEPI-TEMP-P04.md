## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

A 30-year-old right-handed South Asian woman with 2.5 years of stereotyped seizures (rising fear, burning-plastic smell, impaired awareness with right-hand automatisms, left arm dystonic posturing and leftward head turning, occasional bilateral tonic-clonic seizures) has failed levetiracetam and lamotrigine and was labelled PNES elsewhere because two MRIs were normal; she cannot tolerate a 3T MRI (claustrophobia, general anesthesia declined), so an MRI order now returns "not completed" and the non-contrast CT reports CT findings only. The scored work-up is the 6-day video-EEG (six habitual seizures with right anterior temporal onset, now with their semiology), interictal FDG-PET (right temporal hypometabolism) and the drug check (ethinylestradiol halves lamotrigine; zonisamide contraindicated by the sulfonamide allergy), with neuropsychology recommended and CT and literature optional. Musts: presurgical referral, contraceptive interaction handling, contraception and folic acid, a non-inducing adjunct if needed, HLA-B*15:02 before carbamazepine or oxcarbazepine, SUDEP counselling and driving advice. The differential reflects the video-EEG result and every prolactin claim is gone.
