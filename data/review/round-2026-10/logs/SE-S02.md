Post-treatment EEG, the orphan CSF result and the surgery literature are gone. Musts: one more benzodiazepine dose, levetiracetam 60 mg/kg or lacosamide, avoiding phenytoin, fosphenytoin and valproate; cEEG; refractory escalation; continue maintenance; epilepsy-surgery referral.
