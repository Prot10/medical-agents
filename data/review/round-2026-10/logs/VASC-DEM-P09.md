## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing
Diagnosis unchanged (strategic-infarct vascular dementia, bilateral paramedian thalamic and rostral midbrain infarction in the artery of Percheron territory). The exam no longer gives the MoCA; the screen now adds up to 20/30 and lists only scores. The MRI, neuropsychology and screen reports no longer explain the syndrome or exclude alternatives; the MRI adds SWI (no microbleeds or siderosis), normal mamillary bodies and no midbrain atrophy. The MRA no longer claims to show the Percheron artery: it shows no basilar, basilar tip or P1 stenosis and states that perforators are below its resolution. Because the infarct is ischaemic and possibly embolic, an ECG is recommended (normal sinus rhythm) and TTE and a 14-day monitor are optional (both normal), instead of being scored as waste. The literature step is gone, and the optional drug check now covers zolpidem (Beers avoid, 10 mg above the labelled dose for women) and the antiplatelet to be started. Musts now include secondary prevention (single antiplatelet, atorvastatin 80 mg for LDL 106, BP below 130/80), stopping zolpidem, supervised medicines, and work and driving advice. Encephalopathy and primary hypersomnia are now low in the differential.
