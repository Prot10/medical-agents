## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing
The HPI now counts his days: headache on about 8-10 days a month, co-codamol on 6-8 (for attacks after the rizatriptan runs out and the day after), rizatriptan on up to 4, so acute medication on 10-12 days. He is therefore at risk of medication-overuse headache but does not meet ICHD-3 8.2 (which needs 15 headache days), and the differential grades MOH and chronic migraine low instead of high and moderate; the label ("medication-overuse risk") already said so. The ICHD-3 assessment is written for him. The pathway is the assessment, the required rizatriptan-propranolol-topiramate check (rizatriptan 5 mg with propranolol, additive BP lowering with amlodipine), and an optional general MOH search; the preventive and caffeine searches with patient conclusions and the WMH/lipid red herring without data are gone. Musts: stop co-codamol and use naproxen, rizatriptan at headache onset with acute days under 10, prevention (propranolol with rizatriptan 5 mg, or candesartan, topiramate, amitriptyline), caffeine taper, sleep and a diary. Prohibitions: opioid analgesics for migraine; rizatriptan 10 mg with propranolol.
