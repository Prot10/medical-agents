- Mechanism: the label keeps "pregnancy-associated hypercoagulability". A right V3 plus basilar occlusion with headache in a young woman also raises vertebral dissection (CTA cannot fully exclude it in an occluded segment) and paradoxical embolism (bubble study pending); both are in the differential as low. If you want the mechanism settled, a fat-saturated T1 neck MRI and a bubble study result would be needed.
- Antithrombotic choice: low-dose aspirin (second trimester) with prophylactic LMWH, therapeutic LMWH only for a defined indication. Some clinicians would choose therapeutic LMWH for an in situ basilar thrombosis in pregnancy; both are used in pregnancy-associated stroke, and the choice should be confirmed by a reviewer against the ESO 2022 text.
