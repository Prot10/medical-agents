## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing
SE-P01 keeps its diagnosis and story: a 52-year-old heavy drinker with a 3-week subacute decline. Right temporal-occipital LPDs+F on the ictal-interictal continuum resolve, with clinical improvement, during an IV benzodiazepine trial (Salzburg). This is anti-Hu limbic encephalitis with a 4.2 cm hilar mass. The CC and HPI no longer give away the EEG, and the ictal gaze is now leftward. Anti-Hu moved from the first-line labs to its own required antibody-panel step, which also reports a negative neural surface panel (GABA-B included). The first-line labs are a real CMP with renal function, INR and ammonia, without beta-hCG or the exclusion block. The FDG-PET step (note 53545e21) and the blood cultures (note 0dd7682a) are deleted. Tissue diagnosis by EBUS and oncology referral are musts. Thiamine is 500 mg tid, and glucose is never delayed for it. Acyclovir is stopped after a negative PCR. Levetiracetam is preferred over valproate because of the liver disease. The post-correction thiamine panel is gone. Literature and the drug check are optional. The differential is ranked against the results.
