## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

The required work-up is unchanged (informant-based clinical assessment, neuropsychological battery, reversible-cause blood tests, MRI brain without contrast), now listed first; FDG-PET (with perfusion SPECT as an equivalent alternative), a sertraline interaction check and a literature search are optional and last. The clinical assessment now scores all six Rascovsky features as present from the wife's history (disinhibition, apathy, loss of empathy, stereotyped routines, a fixed food preference, an executive-predominant MoCA), with decline in self-care, and concludes possible bvFTD pending imaging and testing. The blood panel keeps every stored value and adds the missing routine analytes (all normal); copper, heavy metals and free T4 were removed, and syphilis/HIV serology (non-reactive) is served only when ordered. The neuropsychology report now gives a letter fluency of 18 as impaired and states memory as low average with preserved recognition. The SPECT report mirrors the FDG-PET pattern. The CT fallback shows the same bifrontal atrophy as the MRI. Musts are: apply Rascovsky with the certainty level; explain why late-onset depression does not explain the syndrome; address driving, finances and lasting power of attorney; caregiver support and a behavioural plan without cholinesterase inhibitors or memantine. The differential rates depression, Alzheimer disease and vascular impairment low after the work-up.
