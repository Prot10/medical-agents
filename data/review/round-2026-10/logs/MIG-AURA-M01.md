## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

The case is a 35-year-old woman on Microgynon with her first and only attack of a right-sided positive sensory aura (10-12 minute march, 20-25 minutes) followed by migrainous headache. Because it is a single attack, the label is now probable migraine with aura (ICHD-3 1.5.2); the structured history output applies criteria A to D to her data (A not met, C 5 of 6) and names the one red flag (new headache type). The brain MRI (recommended) reports no infarct and two punctate non-specific WMH, with the MRA sentence removed. The drug check, required because she takes a combined pill, is now orderable by drug name and states UKMEC/WHO MEC category 4 with the safe alternatives. Literature is optional with one general, correctly cited output. The musts are: stop the combined pill and switch to a progestogen-only or intrauterine method without a gap; tell patient and GP this was aura, not TIA (no TIA pathway, no antiplatelet); sumatriptan at headache onset (not during aura) with or without ibuprofen; diary and review, no preventive after one attack. The "normal DWI excludes TIA" error, the ergot boilerplate, the MRI must, the EEG claim in the differential and the engineering text are gone.
