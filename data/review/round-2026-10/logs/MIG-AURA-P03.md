## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing
38-year-old woman with 10 years of migraine with visual aura, seen 4 weeks after an attack whose left-field aura persisted about 5 hours (previously always under 30 minutes); an outside MRI 3 days later was reported to her as a small stroke and the local team started aspirin plus clopidogrel; she still uses sumatriptan and a progestogen-only pill. The HPI no longer quotes the outside report or reads the images, so the repeat MRI (required) now carries the infarct: 4 mm subacute right occipital cortical infarct in the PCA territory at the site of the earlier DWI lesion. The stroke-mechanism work-up is complete and negative: MRA head and neck (new, required), 30-day event monitor (now a 30-day report), bubble TTE (structured, no shunt) and antiphospholipid antibodies with real values (recommended). Musts: explain migrainous infarction (ICHD-3 1.4.3), stop sumatriptan, single antiplatelet long term (DAPT started late), prevention with the topiramate PPP text, contraception review (POP UKMEC 3 after stroke, copper IUD 1), DVLA 1-month rule. The invented EHF 2022 "cautious triptan" text is gone, and the drug check, literature and musts agree that triptans are contraindicated.
