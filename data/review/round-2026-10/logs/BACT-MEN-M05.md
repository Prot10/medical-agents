Only labs are given upfront (now with HIV non-reactive and normal liver tests). Constraints are soft only: blood cultures and EVD sampling before the CT. IDSA 2017 governs throughout; the WHO line and the literature step are gone. Musts: vancomycin plus cefepime or meropenem within 60 minutes, EVD removal or replacement, imaging without delaying antibiotics, renal and level monitoring, narrowing to nafcillin or oxacillin for MSSA. The history no longer names the differential or the ethnicity, and chemoradiation is planned rather than running in the first postoperative week. The differential is updated to the results.
