## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

A 59-year-old man on dexamethasone three months after resection and radiochemotherapy of a sinonasal adenocarcinoma with skull-base infiltration has ten days of confusion and memory loss, low-grade fever and increasingly frequent staring spells with oral automatisms and fluctuating consciousness. The patient block is unchanged except for the admission timeline, metformin-treated diabetes and a left (not right) facial asymmetry. The contrast MRI now has technique and comparison lines and shows asymmetric right mesial temporal, temporal pole and insular swelling with restricted diffusion and petechial susceptibility, milder left temporal pole signal, no recurrence and patent cisterns. The CSF now shows what HSV encephalitis produces (64 cells/uL lymphocytic, protein 94, red cells, normal glucose ratio, HSV-1 PCR detected, cytology and antibody panel negative). The continuous EEG is written in ACNS 2021 terms: 26 right temporal seizures in 24 hours meeting electrographic status criteria, right temporal LPDs+F; a routine EEG order now gets its own 30-minute report. Required: contrast MRI, continuous EEG, CSF HSV PCR (hard MRI before LP); recommended: renal function, glucose and electrolytes, and the drug check (inducers lower dexamethasone; acyclovir renal plan and metformin); optional: routine EEG and literature; CT is no longer waste (a case-consistent CT answers it); FDG-PET is waste; prolactin removed. Musts: immediate acyclovir 10 mg/kg every 8 hours with renal adjustment for 14-21 days, NCSE treatment with a non-inducing ASM under cEEG, renal and glucose management, dexamethasone continued without abrupt stop, oncology involvement, long-term ASM review after the acute phase, driving. The differential is graded after the work-up.

## Open questions for Andrea (judgment calls you could not settle)

None open. Decisions taken and their reasons:
- Added a routine EEG follow-up and an optional criterion, which the plan does not list: without it every EEG order, including a 30-minute routine one, returned the 24-hour continuous report. Optional because a routine EEG is a reasonable first recording but cannot establish or quantify status epilepticus; no constraint between the two EEG types (plan 3.3).
- CSF criterion accepts HSV PCR alone or a multiplex meningitis/encephalitis panel (which includes HSV-1); cytology is in the stored report but not required, because the diagnosis turns on the PCR.
- The FDG-PET report was moved to the case fallback instead of being dropped: it is consistent with the case, and the library's normal PET would contradict an active encephalitis.
- Dexamethasone must: continued without abrupt withdrawal and reviewed with oncology; adjunctive corticosteroids are not an established HSV encephalitis treatment (IDSA 2008), so no escalation is mandated.
