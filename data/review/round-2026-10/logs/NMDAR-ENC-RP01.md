## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

A 61-year-old man with fluent aphasia, behavioural change and drowsiness starting 5 days into mild COVID-19, recast as anti-NMDAR encephalitis that is CSF-positive (anti-GluN1 1:4 on cell-based assay) and serum-negative. The normal CSF cell count, absent oligoclonal bands and near-normal EEG are kept, so the diagnosis rests on the CSF assay. CSF glucose was corrected to a normal ratio (0.60). CSF now reports RT-QuIC and 14-3-3 negative for the CJD-like cortical ribboning. The MRI no longer reports "restricted diffusion with normal ADC" (now DWI high, ADC not reduced). Required: serum labs including onconeural and neuronal-surface panels, MRI before LP (soft), CSF with RT-QuIC, EEG and contrast CT of the chest, abdomen and pelvis (no tumour); the interaction check and literature are optional. Musts: aciclovir until PCR, steroids with IVIG or plasma exchange and glucose monitoring on metformin, repeat tumour screening if refractory, ward admission, escalation at 10-14 days, and speech and language therapy. The testicular ultrasound, urology consult, ICU and valproate musts are gone. Literature outputs now cite real sources. The differential lists SARS-CoV-2 encephalopathy, HSV/VZV, CJD, other neuronal-surface encephalitis, stroke, osmotic demyelination, NCSE and depression, all low with the excluding data.
