## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

The policy is MRI required (any protocol), with an optional DaTscan (the clinical picture is clear, so NICE NG71 does not ask for it), optional video-PSG and an optional medication review; the literature step is gone. The DaTscan now shows the deficit in the left putamen, opposite his right-sided signs. The MRI no longer reports a right hand-knob thinning or a cervical finding, and the examination no longer has an unexplained right first dorsal interosseous wasting with grip weakness; the postural tremor is bilateral, as coexisting ET requires. The medication review no longer claims that propranolol or sertraline interferes with levodopa: it notes that propranolol blunts the heart-rate response to standing and that sertraline matters for a future MAO-B inhibitor. Musts: clinically probable PD (one supportive criterion, rest tremor), levodopa 150 rising to 300 mg/day with the agonist warning, continue propranolol with lying and standing BP, dream-enactment safety, mood, constipation and physiotherapy. ET is low and coexisting; cervical spondylosis is in the differential and the red herrings, excluded by the examination.
