The MRI now places the nigrosome-1 loss on the right (contralateral to the worse side) in its own finding and loses the enhancement and vascular lines. The policy is a required MRI, neuropsychological battery and medication review; optional blood count and iron studies, lying and standing blood pressure and video-PSG; literature is gone; DaTscan, autonomic and tilt testing, EEG, CSF, echo, monitoring, amyloid, FDG and ECG are waste, with stored DaTscan, MIBG, PSG and orthostatic reports so the library does not contradict the case. The battery's Trail Making B is corrected so that "preserved set-shifting" is true, and its impression gives a multiple-domain MCI, not dementia. The musts are: established PD; separate the iron from levodopa and investigate the anaemia before surgery; a formal levodopa challenge for candidacy; PD-MCI does not exclude DBS, with mood and RBD safety; and the peri-operative drug plan (rasagiline, lorazepam taper, no abrupt dopaminergic withdrawal, agonist ICD questions).
