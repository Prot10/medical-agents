## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

The case is a 46-year-old woman with four stereotyped attacks of expressive language disturbance followed by migraine headache, wrongly anticoagulated with warfarin for presumed TIA and now over-anticoagulated (INR 3.8) with gum and nose bleeding. The pathway now reads: structured ICHD-3 history (required; it records a language aura building over about 5 minutes, 15-25 min, headache within 10 min, no seizure features, 4 of 6 characteristics, and names the red flags), brain MRI with DWI and SWI (recommended; no infarct, no haemorrhage or microbleeds, three unchanged non-specific white matter foci; the MRA text is gone), a 14-day ambulatory monitor (recommended, new; sinus rhythm and atrial ectopy at her palpitations, no AF), the warfarin interaction check (required; paracetamol at regular doses, NSAIDs and aspirin; triptans do not interact) and an optional literature search with real sources. The HPI no longer quotes the outside radiologist. The musts are: correct the diagnosis in writing, stop warfarin without bridging or antiplatelet (her CHA2DS2-VA is 0 once the attacks are recognised as aura, ESC 2024), manage the INR by bleeding severity (Tran 2013 Australasian consensus), triptan at headache onset with NSAIDs only after warfarin has stopped, no preventive at four attacks a year with a diary, and the conditional HRT must (she has been menopausal since 44). The wrong prohibition of combined HRT, the TOE prohibition and the ergot boilerplate are gone; the TOE avoided action is waste. TIA is now low in the differential, and the invented literature references were removed.
