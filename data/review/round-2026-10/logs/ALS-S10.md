## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing
The work-up now reads EMG/NCS, brain MRI, spine MRI and blood tests first, then respiratory function, the gene panel and the riluzole check; the literature step is gone. The EMG report states active denervation in all four regions, chronic neurogenic change in the hand muscles, reduced median and ulnar CMAPs with no conduction block in any nerve tested, and normal sensory studies, without criteria judgements. The labs are standard panels with the stored values and numeric thyroid tests; CK 1240 U/L is reported as 7.1x ULN and the teaching point and differential say this is higher than usual in ALS and that the neurogenic EMG argues against a primary myopathy. The gene panel reports all five genes with a pathogenic C9orf72 expansion; the differential no longer keeps sporadic ALS high, and other familial genotypes are very low. The riluzole check no longer claims caffeine induces CYP1A2 or offers AMX0035 or trials. Musts are management: diagnosis and disclosure as C9orf72-associated familial ALS, 3-monthly respiratory monitoring, riluzole with monitoring, MDT, post-test counselling including FTD risk, counselling of first-degree relatives, baseline cognitive and behavioural screening, and C9orf72 trial eligibility.
