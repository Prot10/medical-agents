## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

The exam now matches the right hemispheric lesion (leftward ictal gaze, left hemiparesis and extensor plantar). The drug check now gives the correct interaction (enzyme inducers lower dexamethasone, not temozolomide) and is required. Blood cultures, post-treatment levels and EEG, the orphan antibody panel and the leaking literature text are gone. Musts: lorazepam, a levetiracetam top-up load avoiding fosphenytoin, cEEG, dexamethasone back to at least 12 mg/day with glucose control, neuro-oncology and neurosurgery.
