## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing

A 47-year-old right-handed man with a left corona radiata lacunar infarct 2 hours after onset (NIHSS 4: right arm 1, right leg 1, sensory 1, dysarthria 1; the exam now states the drifts and that limb clumsiness is in proportion to weakness) now has an explicit acute decision: IV thrombolysis for a disabling deficit of the dominant hand (CADASIL is not a contraindication; BP 148/86 needs no lowering), no thrombectomy (no LVO on CTA). The work-up reads NCCT, labs, ECG, CTA, MRI (recommended; it shows the anterior temporal and external capsule pattern), TTE and 24-hour stroke-unit monitoring (recommended), a young-stroke screen for antiphospholipid antibodies, homocysteine and vasculitis (new follow-up, all normal; the leaking "Exclusion studies" panel and factor V Leiden/protein C/S are gone), NOTCH3 testing after counselling (required; PP1 and the placeholder ClinVar ID removed), then the drug check, which now says sumatriptan is contraindicated after stroke. Musts: thrombolysis with door-to-needle under 60 minutes and BP below 180/105 for 24 hours, stroke unit telemetry, single antiplatelet after 24 hours (no DAPT, no anticoagulation), high-intensity statin for LDL 122, BP below 130/80 long term, stop sumatriptan permanently, genetic counselling and cascade testing. The migraine red herring and pearl now say that migraine without aura is unspecific and that aura is the typical CADASIL symptom. Hypertensive small vessel disease is low in the differential. One literature output remains, optional, with real sources.

The mechanism diagnosis of this case rests on the MRI topography (anterior temporal poles, external capsules, old lacunes); the July review's "MRI not mandatory" concerns the acute reperfusion decision, which the MRI does not delay. Not required, because NOTCH3 testing can be justified from the CT white matter disease and the family history alone.

- The NOTCH3 classification keeps PS1 as the reviewer asked; strictly PS1 is for a different nucleotide change with the same amino acid change, and for this exact variant PS4 or PM1 (cysteine-altering, EGFr domain) are the usual codes. Not changed.
