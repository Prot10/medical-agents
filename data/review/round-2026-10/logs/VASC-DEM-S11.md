The MRI, ECG, neuropsychology and duplex reports state only their own findings. The acute work-up (72-hour telemetry, normal TTE) is now in the history, which is what makes echo and monitoring waste. Labs have male ranges and values consistent with eGFR and the lipid panel. Post-stroke depression is now low. Musts: CEA referral, continue clopidogrel, BP below 130/80, LDL below 70 with ezetimibe or rosuvastatin 40 mg, HbA1c individualised, cognitive rehabilitation, no driving until assessed, reassessment.
