## Changes, in one paragraph a physician can read before re-reviewing
SE-M07 keeps its story: delayed non-convulsive SE five days after a VF arrest, in a woman who woke, was extubated, was discharged on day 4, and then had staring spells and a convulsion without return to baseline. Her results previously described a catastrophic, non-survivable anoxic injury that her clinical course rules out: burst-suppression without reactivity, bilaterally absent N20, cortical laminar necrosis and diffuse edema. They now match her course. CT is normal. Continuous video-EEG shows generalized spike-and-wave and polyspike-and-wave at 2.5-3.5 Hz with time-locked arm myoclonus on a reactive slow background (NCSE by Salzburg). MRI shows bilateral hippocampal restricted diffusion only. The SSEP step, NSE, the template exclusion block and the meningitis blood cultures are gone. The ECG is a plain read with QTc 519 ms. The drug check now gives the correct fosphenytoin-amiodarone interaction (CYP2C9 and conduction risk with metoprolol, not QT) and the lacosamide PR effect. The musts are a benzodiazepine, levetiracetam 60 mg/kg, cEEG, ICD interrogation with telemetry, and ICU care with the refractory step. The prohibitions forbid fosphenytoin, QT-prolonging drugs, early anaesthetic coma, and limiting care on a post-anoxic prognosis, which does not apply to a patient who woke. The pathway order is labs, CT, EEG, ECG, MRI, drug check, literature (optional).

## Open questions for Andrea (judgment calls you could not settle)
None open. One deviation from the plan, decided here: the plan kept the SSEP step "recommended" and called for "multimodal prognostication" musts. ERC-ESICM 2021 [ERC_ESICM_2021] limits prognostication to patients who remain comatose 72 h or more after ROSC with sedation cleared. This patient woke and was discharged, and her present GCS 9 comes from the NCSE. SSEP and NSE therefore have no indication. The absent-N20 result was also physiologically incompatible with her exam. I deleted the SSEP criterion and turned prognostication into a prohibition on limiting care. The lead should know about this for consistency with P03, which is a comatose post-arrest case where SSEP does apply.
