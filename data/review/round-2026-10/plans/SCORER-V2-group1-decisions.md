# Scorer v2, group1: rejected labels and mixed components (25 Oct 2026)

Conditions ALS, FND, HEP-ENC, MS-RR, SAH (150 cases). Brief: `data/review/round-2026-10/SCORER_V2_BRIEF.md`.
Only `ground_truth.diagnosis.rejected` and `required_components` were written (through `case_io.save`);
a field-by-field comparison against HEAD shows no other change. 150 cases changed, 1811 rejected labels
(about 12 per case, above the brief's typical 2 to 8: most cases list 5 to 9 low-likelihood mimics, and each
mimic gets its name plus the acronym or spelling variant a clinician would type). Components set on 5 cases.

Checks: `test_case_guards.py -k diagnosis_label` for the five conditions passes (150); the `case_io check`
suite passes (5327 passed); sanity script (scratchpad `scorer/g1/sanity.py`): every `accepted[0]` and every
short accepted label still scores, except the single-entity labels the components block on purpose
(HEP-ENC-P05, P06, P09, SAH-P05). Each differential's full name, and "accepted or differential" hedges, fail,
except the differentials that are true in the case (concurrent, contributor, known history), the generic
ones ("structural lesion", "spinal cord lesion") and the ones listed under doubts.

## How the choices were made
- Competing diagnoses from the differential, as their short name plus a common acronym (NMOSD, MOGAD, ADEM,
  RCVS, CVST, PML, MMN, IBM, SBMA, NCSE, HRS, SBP, PSP). Never "MG" (it is also milligrams).
- Wrong stage or variant: secondary/primary progressive, SPMS/PPMS and CIS in every RRMS case; covert/minimal HE
  in every overt HE case and overt HE in the covert case (P07); definite/probable ALS where criteria are not met
  (P08); familial vs sporadic ALS (S07, S10); traumatic, perimesencephalic and non-aneurysmal SAH in aneurysmal
  cases, aneurysmal and ruptured aneurysm in the perimesencephalic case (P02).
- In HEP-ENC a wrong precipitant (SBP where the tap is negative, alcohol withdrawal where abstinent, HRS where
  AKI is pre-renal) is rejected like a wrong organism in meningitis; precipitants and contributors named in the
  primary label are never rejected.
- Not rejected: anything the primary label names (concurrent disease, precipitant, earlier misdiagnosis);
  known history (RRMS in ALS-M08 and SAH-RP11, bvFTD in ALS-P09, post-polio syndrome in ALS-P07, TLE in FND-M06,
  IIH in SAH-P07, PD in FND-P07, SLE, migraine and anxiety); moderate "possibly coexisting" or "contributor"
  differentials.
- Components only where the case's answer is two current entities: HEP-ENC-P04 (Wilson + HE), P05 (Wernicke +
  HE), P06 (NCSE + HE), P09 (HE + chronic SDH), SAH-P05 (SAH + eclampsia/PRES). Known-history comorbidities
  (ALS-M08, ALS-P09, FND-M06) got no components.

## Class-level rules for the synonym YAML (not edited)
- G35 (RRMS): reject secondary progressive, primary progressive, SPMS, PPMS, clinically isolated syndrome, CIS,
  NMOSD, neuromyelitis optica, MOGAD.
- K72.90 (overt HE): reject covert/minimal hepatic encephalopathy; K72.90 used for a covert case (P07) should
  reject overt HE.
- I60.0-I60.7 and I60.9 (aneurysmal SAH): reject RCVS, reversible cerebral vasoconstriction syndrome, perimesencephalic,
  non-aneurysmal, traumatic SAH; I60.8 (perimesencephalic, SAH-P02): reject aneurysmal, ruptured aneurysm.
- F44.4-F44.7 (FND): reject epileptic seizures where the code is F44.5 and the case has no epilepsy.

## Doubts for the physicians
1. ALS-P02: the accepted list (from E05.x) contains "graves disease" and "graves hyperthyroidism", while the
   differential "persistent or recurrent Graves hyperthyroidism after radioiodine" is a competitor; "thyrotoxicosis
   or Graves recurrence" therefore scores. Suggest dropping the Graves labels from this case's accepted list.
2. ALS-P03, FND-RP01, HEP-ENC-P01/P08: the primary label names the earlier misdiagnosis (MMN, posttraumatic
   epilepsy, MCI, TIA), so that name cannot be rejected and a hedge with it scores. Suggest moving the
   misdiagnosis out of the label, or a scorer cue for "misdiagnosed as" / "mislabelled as".
3. HEP-ENC-S02, S03, S05: the primary says SBP "requires exclusion" although the tap excludes it (PMN 124, 186,
   162); only the spelled-out name is rejected. Suggest "SBP excluded" in the label, then reject "SBP" too.
4. HEP-ENC-M04: Wernicke is high in the differential and the label says "high Wernicke risk"; Wernicke cannot be
   rejected (the label's tokens contain it). Is the case Wernicke-concurrent (components, like P05) or not?
5. HEP-ENC-P06 and SAH-P05 now require the second entity (HE; eclampsia/PRES): an answer of NCSE alone or
   aneurysmal SAH alone gets no diagnosis credit. Confirm both are meant as mixed diagnoses.
6. ALS-P09 (ALS-FTD) and FND-M06 (PNES + TLE): left without components because the second entity is
   established history. If the label is meant to require it, add components.
7. Scorer limits seen while choosing: "rule out X", "X negative" and "mimicking X" are not denials, so short
   rejected labels (epilepsy, glioma, lymphoma, Wernicke, hypoglycemia, SBP) also fire on such phrasings; and
   matching is a token set over the whole answer, so "wernicke encephalopathy" in an HE case behaves like
   "wernicke".
8. ALS-S10: "ALS due to another familial gene (SOD1, TARDBP, FUS, TBK1)" is not blocked, since gene names occur
   in correct answers ("SOD1 negative").
9. ALS-P08: "suspected amyotrophic lateral sclerosis" does not score (accepted has only "suspected ALS");
   a synonym gap, not a rejection.
