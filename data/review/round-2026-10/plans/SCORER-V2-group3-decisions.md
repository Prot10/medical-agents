## Doubts for the physicians

1. Scorer limits seen here: 'without' is not a denial cue ('sinusitis without meningitis' and 'meningococcal septicaemia without meningitis' match the accepted label; BACT-MEN-RP01, S05), and the token bag makes antibody-status rejections unsafe ('AChR and MuSK negative ... SFEMG positive' contains 'AChR' and 'positive'). MuSK vs AChR vs seronegative MG is therefore not policed (MG-P01, P04, RP14, RS14, RS15).
2. Generalised MG cases do not reject 'ocular myasthenia gravis' (an answer 'generalised MG with ocular onset' would be vetoed; MG-RM16 lists it as a differential).
3. Prior wrong labels kept as rejected (GBS-RS11/RS18 Bell's palsy, NPH-P07 PSP, NPH-P09 Alzheimer, SYNC-CARD-P04 epilepsy, RP01 eclampsia, M05 panic attack, MG-P01 FND, MG-P03 MS relapse, BACT-MEN-RP03 cryptococcal): an answer written as 'X, previously misdiagnosed as Y' would fail. Accept, or drop these?
4. BACT-MEN-P02 (TB meningitis): 'bacterial meningitis' rejected as shorthand for pyogenic meningitis. Mycobacteria are bacteria; confirm.
5. BACT-MEN-RP01 (undiagnosed CVID) and RP04 (cribriform CSF fistula): should the predisposing cause be a required component? Left as single-entity for now.
6. Moderate-likelihood causes of complete heart block (LMNA/SCN5A, cardiac sarcoid in RS01; inherited conduction disease in RP04, RM03) not rejected: they are possible causes, not competitors.
7. NPH iNPH cases reject 'vascular cognitive impairment' even where the differential is moderate and a vascular contribution 'remains possible' (M01-M03, P08, P09, S06, S09, S11).
8. MG-RS14: PSP rejected despite coexisting parkinsonism with downgaze restriction; confirm the parkinsonism is not PSP.
