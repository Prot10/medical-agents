# Scorer v2 rejected labels and components: group 4 (FEPI-TEMP, GLIO-HG, MIG-AURA, PD, VASC-DEM)

25 Oct 2026, per `data/review/round-2026-10/SCORER_V2_BRIEF.md`. Only `ground_truth.diagnosis.rejected` and `required_components` were edited, through `case_io.save`. Guard `diagnosis_label` passes for all 150 cases; sanity script (primary and short labels score, differential names do not) and phrasing probes run. Not committed.

## Shared rules used

- FEPI-TEMP: PNES set (PNES, psychogenic, functional/dissociative seizures, nonepileptic), syncope as two-word forms ("syncope" alone is too ambiguous), frontal lobe epilepsy/FLE, IGE/GGE (never "generalized epilepsy": "secondarily generalized" is a correct phrasing), migraine, plus the case's own mimics. Substrate competitors (DNET vs ganglioglioma, FCD IIIb) are kept wherever the case says only histology separates them; HS is rejected only where the hippocampi are normal.
- GLIO-HG: in every IDH-wildtype GBM case: astrocytoma IDH mutant, metastasis/metastases, lymphoma/PCNSL, abscess, tumefactive, demyelinating/demyelination, plus case mimics. In IDH-mutant astrocytoma and DMG: glioblastoma/GBM. Numeric grades and single molecular tokens (k27m, g34) are never used.
- MIG-AURA: migraine without aura in every aura case (no patient has attacks without aura; only relatives), TIA (3 forms), seizure forms, MS, AVM/cavernoma, and the case mimics. Sporadic hemiplegic cases: familial hemiplegic migraine/FHM.
- PD: MSA, PSP, DLB (3 forms), PDD (PD dementia: no PD-diagnosis case is at dementia stage), vascular and drug-induced parkinsonism, essential tremor, unless the case makes them true (coexisting ET, contributing drugs).
- VASC-DEM: in dementia-stage cases, mild cognitive impairment/MCI/mild neurocognitive disorder/mild vascular cognitive disorder ("vascular cognitive impairment no dementia" is not used: after the denial it reduces to "vascular cognitive impairment", which P03/P04 accept). Alzheimer/alzheimers in pure vascular cases where AD is in the differential; SIVD forms in multi-infarct and strategic cases; DLB, NPH, FTD per differential.

## Doubts for the physicians

1. "without" is not a denial cue in the scorer (because of "migraine without aura"), so "vascular cognitive impairment without dementia" (VASC-DEM-M01) and "Parkinson's disease without dementia" (all PD cases) are vetoed by the rejected "dementia" / "parkinson disease dementia". Suggest a scorer rule: "without X" denies X unless the label itself holds "without".
2. MIG-AURA-P02 is the only components case (migraine with aphasic aura + paroxysmal AF). Same pattern left without components: FEPI-TEMP-RP02 (concurrent PE), FEPI-TEMP-P03 (PNES with known controlled MTLE), FEPI-TEMP-RS01 (hypothalamic hamartoma + MTS dual pathology, where "isolated MTLE" still scores through the short labels). Confirm.
3. Labels that would veto the case's own accepted[0] could not be rejected: TIA in MIG-AURA-P01/P05/S09, hemiplegic migraine in P08, familial hemiplegic migraine in P09 (only FHM), panic disorder in FEPI-TEMP-P01, depression in VASC-DEM-S02, alcohol-related dementia in VASC-DEM-M02. Shortening those accepted[0] texts (dropping the misdiagnosis history) would let them be rejected.
4. Grade within IDH-mutant astrocytoma (GLIO-HG-M04 grade 4, M05 grade 3) is not enforced: "grade 3/4" tokens collide with sizes. "High grade glioma" also accepts the wrong grade.
5. Substrate hedges kept as correct: DNET vs ganglioglioma (FEPI-TEMP-S02, S03, S06, RM04), low-grade tumour in FCD case M04, CAA in VASC-DEM-P08 (CT only). Confirm these should score.
6. PD-M01-M03 reject drug-induced parkinsonism: "PD with superimposed drug-induced worsening" phrased as "drug-induced parkinsonism" is vetoed.
7. Bag-of-tokens collisions to watch: "vascular parkinsonism" vetoes an answer naming PD plus vascular risk; "frontal lobe epilepsy" vetoes "temporal lobe epilepsy with frontal spread".

## Class rules for the synonym YAML (not applied)

- G43.1 (migraine with aura): reject migraine without aura (unless accepted[0] says "without").
- G20 (PD): reject MSA, PSP, DLB, PDD, vascular parkinsonism.
- C71 GBM IDH-wildtype: reject astrocytoma idh mutant, lymphoma/PCNSL, metastasis, abscess, tumefactive; IDH-mutant astrocytoma: reject glioblastoma, GBM, idh wildtype.
- F01 (vascular dementia, dementia stage): reject mild cognitive impairment, MCI, mild neurocognitive disorder.
- G40.1/G40.2 temporal focal epilepsy: reject PNES, psychogenic, functional seizures, dissociative seizures, nonepileptic, convulsive syncope.
