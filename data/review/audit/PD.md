## Top clinical-correctness flags a human must adjudicate

1. **DaTscan laterality reversed (RM02, RM03, RS01, S02)** — striatal DAT deficit is on the SAME side as the worse clinical signs; in PD the deficit is contralateral. RS01 and S02 even contradict their own "contralateral" wording. Correct fix is to swap the L/R binding ratios / side labels, but that alters objective findings → needs author/clinician sign-off. (10 of the 11 asymmetric cases use the correct contralateral convention, e.g. RP01/RS02/RS03/RS04/S01/S03/S04 — so these 4 are outliers, likely a generation slip.)
2. **RP02 gold answer = idiopathic PD despite a near-complete MSA-P picture** (symmetric DaTscan, poor levodopa response, early severe dysautonomia, axial rigidity, early backward falls, equivocal hot-cross-bun). Contrast RP03/P01 which call MSA on similar data. A clinician may reasonably prefer MSA-P. Highest-stakes adjudication.
3. **RS03 gold answer = idiopathic PD despite multiple atypical red flags** (rapid 4-mo course, early frequent falls, Pisa syndrome, prominent early dysautonomia, early cognitive impairment, no RBD). Adjudicate PD vs atypical parkinsonism.
4. **Phantom metoclopramide in ground_truth (P01, RP01, RP02, RS04)** — differential/red_herrings/critical_actions/difficulty_rationale reference a metoclopramide exposure that the case body explicitly denies. Confusing for reviewers and any agent that reads the rationale.
5. **PD-template critical_action on the MSA/DLB/PSP mimics (P01, P02, P03, RP03)** — "Apply MDS 2015 criteria for Parkinson's disease" is the wrong framing for an MSA/DLB/PSP gold answer; pair with the citation_gap each case already documents (needs MSA/DLB/PSP criteria added to the pack).
6. **P03 tau-PET tracer name self-contradiction** (flortaucipir/AV-1451 vs PI-2620) — pick one tracer.
