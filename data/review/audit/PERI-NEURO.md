## Tally

- **Cases audited:** 30 / 30 (M01–M07, P01–P07, RM11, RM12, RP11, RP12, RS11, RS12, RS13, S01–S09) — every field read in full.
- **Findings by severity:** 9 major, ~22 minor, ~6 nit (several consolidated across multiple cases).
- **Fixed:** 10 findings across 7 files (PERI-NEURO-M01, M03, M05, M07, P02, P03, P05) — all unambiguous mechanical errors: 6 `is_abnormal=true`-on-negative-result boolean errors (+ their contradicting interpretation lines), 1 in-range value wrongly listed abnormal (M01 B12), and 3 residual Kind-1 cross-modality / diagnosis-naming / management leaks in nested imaging report summaries (M05 ×2, P03 ×2 — counted as 4 edits across the M05/P03 rows).
- **Flagged (not fixed):** the remainder — dual-vitals divergence in the R-cases, the P02 CMTX1 inheritance-logic incoherence, derived-summary corruptions (P03/RM12/RS11), borderline Kind-1 in nested report summaries and CSF interpretations, management-as-lab-value notes, diagnosis pre-statement in patient records, the missing IENFD value in P01/M06, and the systematic "(H)"/doubled-unit formatter artifact.

## Top clinical-correctness flags for human adjudication

1. **PERI-NEURO-P02 — CMTX1 family-genetics incoherence (major).** The "Son's Genetic Testing" panel's X-linked inheritance logic is wrong (affected father → "obligate carrier" wife; son risk attributed to mother) and self-contradictory. Needs a clinician to rewrite the counseling narrative.
2. **PERI-NEURO-RP11 (+RM12/RP12/RS11/RS12/RS13) — conflicting duplicate vitals (major/minor).** Each R-case carries two disagreeing vitals blocks; RP11's (120/80 vs 148/88, SpO2 95%) matters most because the narrative leans on the abnormal figures. A canonical block should be chosen.
3. **PERI-NEURO-P01 — HbA1c 5.6% called "prediabetic" (minor-but-pervasive).** 5.6 is below the prediabetes threshold yet flagged abnormal and threaded through the story and a red herring; the framing needs a clinician's call.
4. **PERI-NEURO-P01 / M06 — missing IENFD result on the required gold-standard test.** The small-fiber skin-biopsy followups report no density value, so the confirmatory IENFD number is never delivered.
5. **PERI-NEURO-RS13 / S06 — gold diagnosis pre-stated in the patient record** (POEMS in a med indication; CIDP in PMH). Mild leak in the case body that a reviewer should decide whether to soften.
