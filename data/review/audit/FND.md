## Top clinical-correctness flags for human adjudication

1. **R-subtype taxonomy (all 10 FND R-cases)** — they reverse a prior NON-FND misdiagnosis TO FND, the opposite of the criteria-pack's "R = FND was wrong" definition. Decide whether the pack or the cases should change; the lone true FND mimic (P09/SREAT) is mislocated under a P-prefix.
2. **FND-M03 TOMM 52/50** — impossible score on a 50-item validity test; pick a plausible ≤50 value.
3. **FND-M09 drug-check leak** — "Current symptom episode is FND, not PML" announces the case diagnosis inside check_drug_interactions; clearest single residual leak.
4. **FND-M04 EEG exclusion essays** — "This definitively excludes typical absence seizures" + 3-Hz spike-wave teaching across three EEG reports.
5. **Differential completeness (M05 NPSLE, M08 cord metastasis, P04 cord syndrome)** — the principal serious competitor that justifies the workup is absent from the structured differential in these three cases.
6. **Systematic MRI disease-exclusion leakage** — recurring "demyelination excluded / no vasculitis / no PML pattern / no CJD / no PRES / Wilson disease" annotations in MRI `additional_observations`/`impression` (M05, M07, M09, P01, P02, RP01, RS02, S06, S10, S12). Decide a consistent trim policy (keep morphologic pertinent negatives, drop disease-name verdicts).
